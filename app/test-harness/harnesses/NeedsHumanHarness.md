# Prototype: needs-human questions in the garden panel (s-j3j5bk)

Fake data, client only. Nothing here talks to the daemon.

    pnpm --dir app install          # once
    pnpm --dir app exec vite --port 5199
    open 'http://localhost:5199/test-harness/?component=NeedsHuman'

The black strip at the top is harness chrome, not product:

- **placement** — A puts the queue in a band at the top of the panel;
  B stands it in a rail beside the panel or the board.
- **surface** — the list, the board, or a docked seed tile.
- **bramble withdraws** — the raiser takes back an open question while you
  are looking at it.
- **reset** — back to three waiting.

## What to try

1. Answer the gates question from the queue. Watch the count drop, the row
   lose its marker, and the seed go back to reading as ordinary work.
2. Open `s-fjw5bf` afterwards: the answer stands on the seed and lands on
   the log as an `answer` note, which is what its tender wakes up to.
3. Switch to B and back with the same three questions open.
4. Board view: a seed with a question keeps its column and wears the marker.
5. Answer the last two, and see what the empty rail says.
