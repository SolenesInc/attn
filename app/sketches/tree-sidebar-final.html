Tree sidebar: final prototype

The agreed design with real data from the Default profile: section rules (C), 32px rows, the harness logo carries state, role glyphs always visible, no worktree glyph, hover actions over the trailing column. Click a row or a rule to move the active leaf or the current desktop. Hover a row for its actions and a rule for rename. Today is on the right for comparison.
