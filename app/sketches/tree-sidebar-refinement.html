Tree sidebar refinement: sketches

The non-queue sidebar with real data from the Default profile: eight desktops (slot 8 unused), eleven agents, two seed tiles and a browser tile. A is today, built to the measured receipts (header 34px + 10px group gap, rows 36/38px, 33px of indent, 46px of dead space on the right where the hidden ••• menu sits). B, C and D drop the header row a different way and share one row: a 14px lead, the name, and one trailing column flush to the right edge; hover actions float over that column instead of reserving width.

What each direction decides

  • Current desktop: A boxes the whole group; B and C fill the digit with the accent; D rings the layout map. The active leaf is a filled row with a 2px accent tick on its left edge in B, C and D.
  • Empty desktop: A shows a hollow dot and a full header. B and D show one 20px "empty" row; C shows a dashed digit on its rule and nothing under it. A desktop that holds only a tile (Desktop 6) is not empty: it lists the tile.
  • Selecting a desktop without a leaf: today the header row. B and D: the gutter digit or map is the target (18px and 24px wide, full block height). C: the rule row.
  • Truncation: names take whatever the trailing column leaves; the trailing column holds at most the delegation role glyph or the chief mark (12px), so a name gets ~150px at 240 instead of 55 to 85.
  • Tiles read as their content: the seed's title with a SEED tag, the page host with WEB. The id stays in the tooltip.
