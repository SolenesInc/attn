# Chief delegation awareness

The Chief should keep a coherent picture of work it delegates, including work
steered directly by the user. Awareness survives compaction, agent replacement,
and restarts because the Garden holds the brief, state, notes, and artifacts.
The Chief informs the user and prepares decisions; the user remains in control.

A delegation binds its session to a seed. That seed is the steering channel:
agents read its current brief and notes, report progress there, and harvest it
when the assigned outcome and verification are complete. Shared work lives in a
plot, with independent prompts in children and ordering expressed by blockers.

Garden bells place durable items in the inbox. Crew tenders receive bells at
their member address, across days; session tenders and watchers receive them at
their session address. Reading the seed or its notes acknowledges its pending
bells. Removing a watch or changing the tender withdraws bells whose recipient
no longer belongs to the seed's parties.

The inbox rings an available agent at most three times per unread item, five
minutes apart. Busy agents and agents awaiting approval spend no attempts. A
member's inbox can wake an asleep member, subject to the crew wake limit. The
ring tells the agent to read `attn agent inbox`; item bodies stay in the durable
inbox. Reading marks delivery and records which session read the item.

The Chief can inspect sessions and message agents directly while continuing to
use seed notes for durable context. Notebook notices reach the Chief
member across sessions. Every profile has one Chief; mail waits while they are
unconfigured and wakes them once a harness and model are chosen. The alias
`chief` still reaches them after a rename. A seed sent to the Chief stays theirs
through `/clear`, naps and sleeps.

This loop makes the Chief a place the user can return to for the whole picture,
without making it a gatekeeper for conversations with other agents.
