# Delegation

One agent starts another on a seed, from any harness to any harness, as a full
session the user can open and steer. Every delegation has a seed. Either the
dispatcher gives a brief, and attn plants a seed from it, or the dispatcher
names an existing seed. The delegate tends that seed as its stake, reads the
seed when the delegate starts, and reports there. A role sets the
instructions, harness, model and effort for one type of work. The user keeps
the roles, and agents choose among them. In a handover, a seed and its claim
pass to a fresh agent, and the previous agent keeps running.

## Sub-features

- `delegate-brief`: start a delegate on new work. The brief becomes the delegate's seed. When the dispatcher has a stake, that seed is part of the stake.
- `delegate-seed`: start a delegate on an existing seed nobody active tends.
- `handover`: a new session takes over a seed and its claim, with a note. The previous agent keeps running.
- `roles`: the user's roles, their alternatives and the fallback, kept in revisions with history and rollback.
- `workflow-skill`: "Add Attn roles" or `attn delegate roles install` adds attn's own roles and installs the attn-workflow skill that those roles use. While workflow skills are enabled, the daemon refreshes them at startup, when you save an executable setting, when you save delegation preferences, and when a role launches. Every way of saving preferences counts: the app, a CLI commit, and a rollback. Reading settings does not touch the skill files.
- `delegate-placement`: the delegate opens at the end of the row on the dispatcher's desktop, or on a named desktop, without changing what the user sees.
- `delegate-checkout`: the machine, folder and Git checkout the delegate works in.
- `delegation-status`: a stable request id to check or retry a delegation whose answer was lost.
- `delegation-chain`: the dispatcher, peers and delegates of a session, as a card you walk with the keyboard.
- `jump-to-dispatcher`: go from a delegate to the session that started it.
- `seed-continue`: Resume and Handover on a seed's page.
- `delegation-guidance`: what attn tells dispatchers and delegates, including the plan sign-off before dispatch and independent design candidates.

## How to get to it

### People

- ⌘⇧↑ (`session.orchestrator`, `app/src/shortcuts/registry.ts`) goes to the dispatcher of the agent on screen.
- Palette (`app/src/application/useAppCommands.tsx`): `show-delegation-chain` ("Show delegation chain"), offered while an agent is active.
- [desktops.md](desktops.md) covers moving an agent with its delegates, from the palette or by dragging the agent's sidebar row with ⌥ held.
- The chain trigger on the agent's tile header, which names the role, opens the delegation chain card. On a sidebar row the trigger shows at rest, and the card opens when you point at the row. While the pointer is over the row, the row's actions cover the trigger (`app/src/components/DelegationChain.tsx`). In the card, ↑ ↓ Home End walk the chain, Enter opens an agent, Escape closes the card.
- A seed's page in the garden: "Resume" and "Handover", with the handover form that asks "What should the new agent know?", the working folder, the Git checkout and the agent (`app/src/components/GardenPanel.tsx`, `app/src/components/DelegationCheckoutFields.tsx`).
- **Settings → Delegation** (`app/src/components/DelegationSettings.tsx`): the roles table and its switch, the alternatives, the fallback "Anything else", and "Add Attn roles". "Add Attn roles" also installs the attn-workflow skill for each harness that has a skills folder.
- Where people see and steer what agents do: the delegate is a full session in a tile on its dispatcher's desktop, with the delegate's role on the tile header and the seed the delegate tends on the tile's seed chip. People type into the delegate like any agent.
- Their own shell: `attn delegate --source-session <id>` and `attn delegate roles`, below.

### Agents

- `attn delegate` (`cmd/attnd/main.go`): `--brief`, `--brief-file` or `--seed`. `--handover` with `-m`. `--authorization`. `--cwd` with `--reuse-checkout`, `--new-worktree`, `--branch`, `--existing-branch`, `--from`, `--worktree-path` and `--allow-worktree-reuse`. `--host`. `--role`, `--choice`, `--fallback`, `--agent`, `--model`, `--provider`, `--effort`. `--name`, `--desktop`, `--priority`, `--yolo`, `--request-id`, `--source-session`, `--profile`.
- `attn delegate status <request-or-operation-id>` (`cmd/attnd/main.go`).
- `attn delegate roles` (`cmd/attnd/delegation_roles.go`): with no command, it prints the catalog agents choose from. The commands are `show`, `history`, `install`, `add`, `set`, `copy`, `rm`, `enable`, `disable`, `apply`, `rollback`.
- `attn session list --tree` nests live sessions under their dispatchers (`cmd/attnd/session_ledger.go`).
- `attn seed handover` is gone: it answers `seed: unknown command "handover"`. `attn delegate --seed <id> --handover --cwd <path>` replaces it.
- Prompts: every session's instructions carry the delegation boundary (`internal/prompts/content/delegation-boundary.md`). The delegate's opening names the delegate's seed and the seed's checks, including the shared checks of the seed's plot, or says that the seed has none. When the seed has an authorization, the opening ends with it and the acting rule that follows it (`internal/prompts/content/delegation/identity.md`, `opening.md`, `seed.md`, `seed-checks.md`, `seed-no-checks.md`, `authorization.md`, `authorization-from-user.md`, `authorization-rule.md`). The role's execution guidance and the catalog text are in `internal/prompts/content/delegation-preferences/`. `attn skill --reference delegation`s are `internal/prompts/content/skills/attn/references/delegation.md`, `delegated-agent.md` and `delegation-roles.md`, and its `delivery.md` covers both repository delivery flows. The attn-workflow skill tells a Pathfinder how to hand off a plan and when to run prototypes or independent candidates, and tells an Orchestrator how to delegate Builders (`internal/prompts/content/skills/attn-workflow/SKILL.md`, `references/planning.md`, `references/prototype.md`, `references/orchestration.md`). Without a daemon, `attn prompts show attn-workflow-skill` lists the skill and its references and `attn prompts render attn-workflow-skill planning` prints one ([agent-guidance.md](agent-guidance.md)).

## Contexts

- **Durability across restarts**: a delegation operation, its request id and its seed are the daemon's records. `attn delegate status` reads them after a restart, and a retry with the same `--request-id` returns the accepted operation instead of starting another operation. A delegation that a restart interrupts finishes on the same session once the daemon is back. Delegates come back like any session.
- **Linux**: `attn delegate` works the same where the daemon and CLI run on Linux. `session.orchestrator` is Ctrl+Alt+O there (`LINUX_SHORTCUTS`).
- **Outposts**: a delegation starts on the dispatcher's machine unless `--host` names an outpost, or `home`. attn checks paths on the target machine. attn refuses a delegation to an unknown or unreachable host before it accepts the delegation. After a home restart, an accepted operation waits for its outpost, and its status names the host. attn judges checkout sharing per machine.
- **Profiles**: the delegate, its seed and its desktop belong to the dispatcher's profile. `--desktop` takes only desktops of that profile. Outside attn, `--profile` picks the profile when there are several.
- **Performance and idle cost**: an open delegation chain card must not repaint while idle. The `delegation-chain` scenario samples the app with the card open.
- **Harnesses**: any harness delegates to any harness. `--agent` picks the delegate's harness, `--provider` picks a plugin harness's model provider, and a role's choice pins harness, model and effort. Without a role, `--model` is required. `default` asks for the harness's own model.
- **Keyboard and native input**: ⌘⇧↑, the palette and the card's arrows, Home, End, Enter and Escape reach the chain. A placed delegate does not take the keyboard: the dispatcher keeps focus.
- **Compatibility across versions**: retired flags answer with their replacement: `--ticket`, `--confirm`, `--plot`, `--worktree`, `--repo`, `--no-worktree`, `--preferences-revision` and the two flags that once chose a desktop. A delegate keeps the role it launched with when the role is later changed or deleted.
- **Flow**: `--priority` puts the delegate's owed turns first in queue flow. The chain trigger shows on queue sidebar rows and desktop sidebar rows alike.

## Gotchas

- `bridge capture_screenshot` draws the page without the chain card: read the card with `dom_text` on `[data-testid="delegation-chain-popover"]`.
- `attn delegate status` with an id no request or operation has, such as one the daemon refused before accepting it, answers `sql: no rows in result set` (seed `name-missing-request-attn-delegate-status`, s-9fw162).
- The `bridge` command leaves the app in the background. A card can own `document.activeElement` while CSS `:focus` matches nothing. Use `dom_wait` with `"focused":true` to check the keyboard target.
- In one live macOS drive, a native click reached `[data-testid="settings-nav-delegation"]` (the trusted pointer receipt matched) but left `settings_get_state.activeSection` on `connectivity`.
