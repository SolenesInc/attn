# Crew

Permanent agents with charters, who leave letters for their next session; the
chief coordinates the profile. A crew member is an agent with a name, a
charter that says who they are, and a home folder that keeps the charter and
the letters. A member is asleep until something wakes them: the user, an
agent, or an item in their mailbox. Each session of theirs reads the last
letter and closes by filing a new one, which puts the member to sleep, or,
after a harness change is applied, starts their next session on the new
harness from it. The chief coordinates one profile: seeds sent to the chief
land with it, and it may close or reload any agent of the profile. With crew
v2 off the chief is a session the user marks; with it on, it is a crew member
every profile has.

## Sub-features

- `crew-roster`: the members of the profile, asleep or awake, with their launch settings.
- `crew-create`: add a member to the profile; its key comes from its name and its home starts with an empty charter.
- `crew-retire`: stop a member's work and mail, keeping its name, home and unread mail; `restore` undoes it.
- `crew-rename`: a member's name, unique in its profile, which the user changes with crew v2 on; its key never changes. A home in the Notebook moves to a folder named after the new name, so that rename needs the member asleep.
- `crew-home`: the member's home folder. `attn crew create` makes it in the profile's Notebook at `crew/<name>/`. Homes in attn's data folder, from before or made by hand, stay there until `attn crew move-home` moves one into the Notebook; changing the Notebook folder later leaves homes where they are.
- `crew-charter`: who the member is, kept in `CHARTER.md` in the member home and editable from the app.
- `crew-launch`: the folder, harness, model, effort and awareness folders a member's next session starts with; the launch desktop is in [desktops.md](desktops.md).
- `crew-wake`: start a session for an asleep member; asking an awake member returns its session.
- `crew-sleep`: ask the member's session to file its letter and close.
- `crew-clear`: `/clear` (Claude Code) or `/clear` and `/new` (Codex) in a member's terminal keeps the member awake in the new conversation's session; `attn crew prime` re-reads its orientation there.
- `crew-letter`: the letter a session files with `attn handoff`, which ends the session and leaves the member asleep, or, when an applied harness change asked for it, starts the member's next session on the new harness from it.
- `crew-apply`: put an awake member's saved launch settings and charter into its running session: a reload in place on the same harness, or a letter request on a different one. A requested switch shows as `switch_pending` in `attn crew list --json` and in Manage crew until the letter lands; saving the running harness back or a sleep request cancels it.
- `crew-mailbox`: items addressed to a member reach its current session, and wake it when it sleeps.
- `crew-heartbeat`: attn keeps an idle member's cache warm, and puts it to sleep after the user is away.
- `chief`: the profile's coordinating session; made from the session menu or the new-session picker. With crew v2 off only.
- `chief-member`: with crew v2 on, the Chief is a crew member every profile has. It is created when the switch turns on, at daemon start and with each new profile, prefilled from the session that was chief. `chief` reaches it under any name; it can't be closed or retired, gets no heartbeat or auto-sleep, and the chief's powers follow its current session.
- `chief-slot`: with crew v2 on, the Chief's row at the top of the sidebar's Crew section, in queue and desktop flow. While the Chief has no harness, it opens a setup popover (harness, model, effort, Notebook folder; Enter saves then wakes); otherwise it is the Chief's crew row with one-click wake, Sleep, "Harness and model…" and "Edit charter".
- `send-to-chief`: hand a seed to the chief to decide how it continues.
- `crew-queue`: whether crew members owing a turn wait in the queue.
- `crew-guidance`: what attn tells members and the chief.

## How to get to it

### People

- Palette (`app/src/application/useAppCommands.tsx`): `manage-crew` ("Manage crew"), and `toggle-crew-queue` ("Put the crew in the queue" or "Take the crew out of the queue").
- Sidebar: the "Crew" section with its "manage" button and a row per asleep member whose sun button wakes them on a second click; "Actions for <member>" opens the member's menu (`app/src/components/SidebarChrome.tsx`, `app/src/components/QueueSidebar.tsx`, `app/src/components/QueueRows.tsx`).
- The Manage crew panel (`app/src/components/CrewPanel.tsx`): the member list with "Find a member" (`CrewRoster.tsx`), and per member the tabs "Launch settings" with "Wake" or "Sleep", and "Apply" while the member is awake (`CrewLaunchTab.tsx`), "Charter" (`CrewCharterTab.tsx`), "Handoffs", which lists the letters (`CrewHandoffsTab.tsx`), and "Seeds" (`CrewSeeds.tsx`). With crew v2 on, "Rename" beside the member's name opens a field: Enter saves, Escape cancels. "Close" or Escape closes it.
- The chief: "Make chief of staff" and "Remove chief role" in a session's menu, with a transfer prompt when another session is chief (`app/src/components/SessionActionsPopover.tsx`, `app/src/components/ChiefOfStaffTransferPrompt.tsx`); the chief switch in the new-session picker (`app/src/components/NewSessionDialog/SessionControls.tsx`); the chief badge on its sidebar row.
- The Chief slot, with crew v2 on (`app/src/components/ChiefSlot.tsx`, `ChiefSetup.tsx`, `app/src/application/useChief.ts`): the row in the Crew section; the setup popover beside the sidebar ("Set up Chief" / "Chief's harness", "Wake Chief ⏎", "Save for next wake", "No harness here can run the Chief…" with "Check again" and "Settings › Agents"); "Harness and model…", "Edit charter" and "Wake Chief" or "Sleep Chief" in its menus; the palette's agent row ("not set up" / "asleep") and commands "Set up Chief", "Wake Chief", "Put Chief to sleep", "Change Chief's harness and model". The setup sends `crew_set` with `notebook_root` when the folder changed, then `crew_wake`.
- A seed's page in the garden: "Send to Chief", with "What should Chief know?" (`app/src/components/GardenPanel.tsx`).
- Settings, Background agents: the chief's model, effort and context budget. Crew v2 has no switch in the app: `attn settings set crew_v2_enabled true`.
- Where people see what members do: a member's session is an ordinary agent tile named after the member; their letters are in the panel's "Handoffs" tab and in the member home.
- Their own shell: `attn crew` and `attn seed send-to-chief`, below.

### Agents

- `attn crew` (`cmd/attn/crew.go`): `list`, `wake <member> [--agent <name>]`, `sleep <member>`, `apply <member>`, `set <member>` with `--cwd`, `--agent`, `--model`, `--effort`, `--launch-desktop`, `--desktop-name` and `--awareness-dir`, and `rename <member> <name>` (crew v2 only), `create <name>`, `retire <member>`, `restore <member>`, `move-home <member>`, and `list --all` to include retired members; `--json` and `--profile` on each except `prime`, which takes no flags: run from a member's own terminal, it prints the orientation its session started with, read fresh. A `<member>` is a name in the caller's profile, or `member:<key>`; `attn agent peek`, `msg` and `reload` read member names the same way.
- `attn handoff -m "<letter>"` (`cmd/attn/handoff.go`), with `-m -` to read the letter from stdin, `--retry`, `--session` and `--json`. It files the letter, ends the session and leaves the member asleep, whether or not the user is at the machine. When `attn crew apply` asked this session for its letter because the member's harness changed, it starts the next session on the saved harness instead. After a failed turnover, `--retry` turns over again with the letter already filed; `attn crew sleep` first makes that retry end with the member asleep.
- `attn crew apply <member>` (`cmd/attn/crew.go`, `internal/daemon/crew_apply.go`), allowed for the member itself, an agent above it in the delegation chain, the chief, and the app. On the same harness it reloads the session in place, keeps the conversation and sends a notice to reread the charter; on another harness it sends a notice to file the letter. An asleep member prints `... is already asleep; its next wake uses the saved settings.`
- `attn seed send-to-chief <id> [-m "<guidance>"]` (`cmd/attn/seed.go`), and `attn seed tend <id> --for <name>` to assign a seed to a member.
- `attn agent msg <member> "text"` addresses the member's mailbox; [inbox.md](inbox.md) drives it.
- `attn agent reload <member>` reloads the member's current session in place, under the reload rules in [agent-launch.md](agent-launch.md), with the member's saved model and effort when its harness matches. A sleeping member is refused and stays asleep.
- The Chief member: `attn crew set chief --agent <harness> [--model <model>]` configures a Chief that has no harness yet, `attn crew wake chief` wakes it, and `attn crew list --json` marks it `"chief": true`. Its guidance is part of the crew v2 priming (`internal/prompts/content/crew/v2/chief.md`, and `v2/chief-switchover.md` on the first day of a Chief prefilled from a session). The member doc's `first_day` holds that session until the Chief files its first letter.
- Prompts: `crew_v2_enabled`, read at each launch, picks the set a member gets. Off: the identity, charter or first day, letters, closure and garden in its session instructions (`internal/prompts/content/crew/`), the opening that wakes it (`crew/wake.md`) and the one a successor gets (`crew/successor.md`). On: the v2 set in `internal/prompts/content/crew/v2/` replaces each of those, openings included (`v2/wake.md`, `v2/successor.md`), and adds the compaction note and the operating model "How you work" (`v2/operating-model.md`). Either way: the line before the priming that asks a cleared conversation to run `attn crew prime` (`crew/cleared.md`), the sleep request (`crew/sleep-requested.md`, `crew/sleep-away.md`), the apply notices (`crew/switch-requested.md`, `crew/settings-applied.md`) and the heartbeat (`crew/heartbeat.md`); the chief's guidance (`internal/prompts/content/chief.md`), and its seed assignment (`internal/prompts/content/chief/seed-assignment.md`).
- The attn skill's charter reference, `attn skill --reference charter` (`internal/prompts/content/skills/attn/references/charter.md`), named in its capability index as "Write or update your crew charter" (`internal/prompts/content/skills/attn/SKILL.md`). A v2 member reads it on its first day before writing `CHARTER.md`, and when it adapts its charter to its work. `attn skill` itself is in [agent-guidance.md](agent-guidance.md).

### Triggers

- An item for an asleep member wakes them before the ring, within the wake limit of `crew.wake_limit` wakes per `crew.wake_limit_window_seconds` (`internal/daemon/crew_wake.go`, `internal/daemon/inbox_delivery.go`; rules in `docs/internal/inbox.md`).
- A lifecycle tick every 60 seconds sends an idle member a cache heartbeat before its cache lapses, and asks it to sleep once the user has been away for `crew.away_seconds` (`internal/daemon/crew_lifecycle.go`). `crew.heartbeat_enabled` and `crew.autosleep_enabled` turn each off.

- The baseline instance. The steps that run `bridge` also need its app (`bridge --launch --wait-ready ping` answers) with What's new dismissed (`bridge dismiss_whats_new`); the rest need only the daemon.

## Contexts

- **Durability across restarts**: charters and letters are files in the member home, and the roster, launch settings and bindings are the daemon's records. A daemon crash during a clear still leaves the member awake in the new session. An awake member's session comes back like any session, and unread mailbox items stay for the member's next session.
- **Linux**: `attn crew` and `attn handoff` work the same where the daemon and CLI run on Linux. The crew has no shortcut; the palette reaches it.
- **Outposts**: a member's sessions start on the home's machine; `attn crew set` has no host. A session on an outpost has no "Make chief of staff" in its menu.
- **Profiles**: every member belongs to one profile and wakes only there. Names resolve only in the caller's profile (the calling session's, else `--profile`, else the app's selected profile), so a member of another profile answers as if it did not exist, and two profiles can each have a Bob. Each profile has at most one chief, and a message to a member of another profile answers as if the member did not exist.
- **Performance and idle cost**: the sidebar's wake sun animates to a held frame when armed and stops; a glow that keeps moving is a repaint defect. Each heartbeat is a turn typed into an idle member's session before its cache lapses: 3600 seconds for Claude and 1800 for Codex unless `crew.cache_ttl_seconds` says otherwise, less `crew.heartbeat_lead_seconds`.
- **Harnesses**: `attn crew set --agent` picks claude, codex or an installed plugin driver, and `attn crew wake --agent` overrides it for one session. With `crew_v2_enabled`, the v2 prompts under Agents and harness memory settings apply at the next launch: Claude Code gets "How you work" in its appended system instructions, and Codex, plain or shared, in its developer instructions.
- **Keyboard and native input**: the palette opens the panel, Escape closes it, and the wake sun is a button that Enter or Space arms and then confirms.
- **Compatibility across versions**: crew memory was removed; with `crew_v2_enabled` on, a letter also records its session so attn keeps that session's transcript. The store's crew migrations carry older member records forward. `attn handoff --sleep` and `--nap` are retired and exit 2 with `flag provided but not defined: -sleep` or `-nap`. A member session an earlier build started was told to file with it; plain `attn handoff` now does the same, and from such a session it sleeps even where its instructions promised a successor while the user is present.
- **Flow**: in desktop flow, the sidebar lists asleep members above the desktops. In queue flow, the queue sidebar has a crew block with awake and asleep members, and "Put the crew in the queue" lets a member owing a turn wait in the queue.

## Gotchas

- `attn crew create` adds a member at once. A home folder made by hand joins the roster only when the daemon starts and finds `<key>/CHARTER.md` in the instance's crew folder; one made while the daemon runs stays invisible until the next start. Keys are lowercase letters, digits and `-`, starting with a letter.
- Retired members are left out of the app's roster and of `attn crew list`; only `attn crew list --all` shows them. A retired member's awake session keeps running until it files its letter, and that letter always ends with the member asleep, even with a harness switch pending.
- Both wake openings mention the member's letter, so a fixture turn that matches `letter` fires at wake. Match on words the opening does not carry, as the fixture above does.
- `crew_v2_enabled` changes the openings. The v2 wake starts `You have been woken. Get up to speed` and carries no `woken for today` or `predecessor`; the v2 successor starts `Your previous session filed the letter above` instead of `Your predecessor closed their day`. A turn that matches words only one set carries never fires under the other, and the run silently proves nothing. Match words both carry, such as `You have been woken` and `the letter above`, as the fixture's wake turn does.
- A sleep request is an inbox notice: the member reads it with `attn agent inbox` after the 📬 ring, and the 30 seconds of quiet after any `wsctl input` to that terminal apply. Only the member's own `attn handoff` ends the session; `attn crew sleep` and the panel's Sleep only ask.
- `attn handoff` leaves the member asleep even while the user is at the machine. Only a letter that `attn crew apply` asked for after a harness change starts a successor, so a run that expects one applies a harness change first. `attn handoff --sleep` and `--nap` exit 2 with `flag provided but not defined`, which shows as a `mock agent error:` block while the session stays awake.
- Inbox wakes count against `crew.wake_limit`; a run that wakes a member by message many times hits it, and the receipt names the setting. Wakes from the sidebar or `attn crew wake` do not count.
- With crew v2 off, attn never wakes the chief. Items for the chief wait for the next chief, and on a profile without one `attn seed send-to-chief` exits 1 with `this profile has no Chief`. With crew v2 on, a seed sent to the Chief and mail to `chief` go to the Chief member and wake it like any member, unless it has no harness yet.
- The panel's Wake stays disabled until the launch settings are saved.
- Wake selects the member's session and closes Manage crew. Reopen the panel before looking for its charter or letters. The closed panel's root remains in the DOM with zero-size bounds; its presence alone does not show that the panel is open.
- The CLI and the panel still use older words for a member's session and letters (the "Handoffs" tab, `attn handoff`, the sleep request's "handoff"). Match the literal text shown here; the glossary lists those words as debt, not as a failure of the run.
- Working but wrong: `Asked Fern ...`, `Sleep request for Fern is queued ...` and `Fern's session <id> is busy; it will be asked ...` only say the request was recorded. Fern is asleep when `./attn crew list` says so. Likewise `awake` says a session launched, not that it read its charter: prove a wake by the member's reply in `./attn agent peek fern`.
