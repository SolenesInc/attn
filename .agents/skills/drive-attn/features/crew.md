# Crew

Crew members are permanent agents with charters. Each session leaves a letter
for the next one, and the chief coordinates the profile. A crew member is an
agent with a name, a charter that says who the member is, and a home folder
that keeps the charter and the letters. A member stays asleep until the user or
an agent wakes it, or until an item arrives in the member's mailbox and attn
wakes it. Each session reads the last letter and ends by filing a new one.
Filing the letter puts the member to sleep. After a harness change is applied,
attn instead starts the member's next session on the new harness from the
letter. The chief coordinates one profile. Seeds sent to the chief go to the
chief, and the chief may close or reload any agent of the profile. The chief is
a crew member that every profile has.

## Sub-features

- `crew-roster`: the members of the profile, asleep or awake, with their launch settings.
- `crew-create`: add a member to the profile. The member's key comes from its name, and its home starts with an empty charter.
- `crew-retire`: stop a member's work and mail while keeping its name, home and unread mail. `attn crew restore` reverses the retirement.
- `crew-rename`: a member's name, unique in its profile. The user changes the name, and the member's key never changes. A home in the Notebook moves to a folder named after the new name, at once when the member is asleep and at the member's next wake when the member is awake.
- `crew-home`: the member's home folder. `attn crew create` makes the home in the profile's Notebook at `crew/<name>/`. A home stays where it is until `attn crew move-home` moves it into the profile's current Notebook, from attn's data folder (homes from before or made by hand) or from an earlier Notebook. Changing the Notebook folder moves no home. The user repoints a folder that they moved or renamed with `attn crew set <member> --home <dir>`, which renames the folder after the member. Repointing at `crew/<key>/` in the data folder takes a home back out of the Notebook. After a move, rename or repoint, sessions saved in the home, closed ones included, resume in the new home.
- `crew-charter`: who the member is, kept in `CHARTER.md` in the member home and editable from the app.
- `crew-launch`: the folder, harness, model, effort and awareness folders a member's next session starts with. The launch desktop is in [desktops.md](desktops.md).
- `crew-wake`: start a session for an asleep member. Waking an awake member returns the member's session.
- `crew-sleep`: ask the member's session to file its letter and close.
- `crew-resume`: one session is the member at a time. Opening a member's stopped session in the app (attach with policy revive), `attn session reopen`, a seed's Resume or a reload of a stopped session brings it back as the member while the member is asleep: the session takes the binding and its priming, and a wake names it. While another session of the member is awake, attn refuses and names that session. A retired member's sessions stay closed.
- `crew-clear`: after `/clear` (Claude Code) or `/clear` and `/new` (Codex) in a member's terminal, the member stays awake in the new conversation's session. `attn crew prime` there re-reads the member's orientation.
- `crew-letter`: the letter a session files with `attn handoff`. Filing it ends the session and leaves the member asleep. When `attn crew apply` asked for the letter after a harness change, filing it starts the member's next session on the new harness from the letter.
- `crew-apply`: put an awake member's saved launch settings and charter into the member's running session. On the same harness, apply reloads the session in place. On a different harness, apply requests a letter. A requested switch shows as `switch_pending` in `attn crew list --json` and in Manage crew until the member files the letter. Saving the running harness back, or a sleep request, cancels the switch.
- `crew-mailbox`: attn delivers items addressed to a member to the member's current session and wakes the member when it sleeps.
- `crew-heartbeat`: attn keeps an idle member's cache warm and puts the member to sleep after the user is away.
- `chief-member`: the Chief is a crew member that every profile has. attn creates the Chief at daemon start and with each new profile. On the first start after an upgrade, a profile that had a chief session gets a Chief prefilled from that session. `chief` reaches the Chief under any name. The Chief can't be closed or retired and gets no heartbeat or auto-sleep. It follows the crew's queue rule (`queue_crew_enabled`) and keeps its row. The chief's powers follow the Chief's current session.
- `chief-slot`: the Chief's row at the top of the sidebar's Crew section, in queue and desktop flow. While the Chief has no harness, the row opens a setup popover (harness, model, effort, Notebook folder), where Enter saves and then wakes. Otherwise the row is the Chief's crew row with one-click wake, Sleep, "Harness and model…" and "Edit charter".
- `send-to-chief`: hand a seed to the chief, who decides how the seed continues.
- `crew-queue`: whether crew members that are owed a turn wait in the queue.
- `crew-guidance`: what attn tells members and the chief.

## How to get to it

### People

- Settings → Attention queue (`app/src/components/SettingsModalSections.tsx`): the switch `[data-testid="settings-queue-crew-toggle"]` for the same setting, found by searching "crew".
- Palette (`app/src/application/useAppCommands.tsx`): `manage-crew` ("Manage crew"), and `toggle-crew-queue` ("Put the crew in the queue" or "Take the crew out of the queue").
- Sidebar (`app/src/components/SidebarChrome.tsx`, `app/src/components/QueueSidebar.tsx`, `app/src/components/QueueRows.tsx`): the "Crew" section with its "manage" button and a row per asleep member whose sun button wakes the member on a second click. "Actions for <member>" opens the member's menu.
- The Manage crew panel (`app/src/components/CrewPanel.tsx`): the member list, with "Find a member" once the list holds more than five members (`CrewRoster.tsx`), and per member the tabs "Launch settings" with "Wake" or "Sleep", and "Apply" while the member is awake (`CrewLaunchTab.tsx`), "Charter" (`CrewCharterTab.tsx`), "Handoffs", which lists the letters (`CrewHandoffsTab.tsx`), and "Seeds" (`CrewSeeds.tsx`). "Rename" beside the member's name opens a field where Enter saves and Escape cancels. "Close" or Escape closes the panel.
- The Chief slot (`app/src/components/ChiefSlot.tsx`, `ChiefSetup.tsx`, `app/src/application/useChief.ts`): the row in the Crew section. The setup popover beside the sidebar ("Set up Chief" or "Chief's harness", "Wake Chief ⏎", "Save for next wake", "No harness here can run the Chief…" with "Check again" and "Settings › Agents"). "Harness and model…", "Edit charter" and "Wake Chief" or "Sleep Chief" in the slot's menus. The palette's agent row ("not set up" or "asleep") and commands "Set up Chief", "Wake Chief", "Put Chief to sleep", "Change Chief's harness and model". The setup sends `crew_set` with `notebook_root` when the folder changed, then `crew_wake`.
- A seed's page in the garden: "Send to Chief", with "What should Chief know?" (`app/src/components/GardenPanel.tsx`).
- **Settings → Background agents**: the chief's model, effort and context budget. No launch reads them.
- Where people see what members do: a member's session is an ordinary agent tile named after the member. The member's letters are in the panel's "Handoffs" tab and in the member home.
- The phone app's Crew section: tap an asleep member to wake it ([phones.md](phones.md)).
- Their own shell: `attn crew` and `attn seed send-to-chief`, below.

### Agents

- `attn crew` (`cli/attn/src/commands/crew.rs`): `list` (with `--all` to include retired members), `wake <member> [--agent <name>]`, `sleep <member>`, `apply <member>`, `set <member>`, `rename <member> <name>`, `create <name>`, `retire <member>`, `restore <member>`, `move-home <member>` and `prime`. `set` takes `--cwd`, `--agent`, `--model`, `--effort`, `--launch-desktop`, `--desktop-name`, `--awareness-dir` and `--home`. Each command except `prime` takes `--json` and `--profile`. `prime` takes no flags. Run `prime` from a member's own terminal. It prints the orientation that the member's session started with, read fresh from source. A `<member>` is a name in the caller's profile, or `member:<key>`. `attn agent peek`, `msg` and `reload` read member names the same way.
- `attn handoff -m "<letter>"` (`cmd/attnd/handoff.go`), with `-m -` to read the letter from stdin, `--retry`, `--session` and `--json`. It files the letter, ends the session and leaves the member asleep, whether or not the user is at the machine. When `attn crew apply` asked this session for its letter because the member's harness changed, `attn handoff` starts the next session on the saved harness instead. After a failed turnover, `--retry` turns over again with the letter already filed. Run `attn crew sleep` first to make that retry end with the member asleep.
- `attn crew apply <member>` (`cli/attn/src/commands/crew.rs`, `internal/daemon/crew_apply.go`), allowed for the member itself, an agent above the member in the delegation chain, the chief, and the app. On the same harness, the command reloads the session in place, keeps the conversation and sends a notice to reread the charter. On another harness, it sends a notice to file the letter. For an asleep member, it prints `... is already asleep; its next wake uses the saved settings.`
- `attn seed send-to-chief <id> [-m "<guidance>"]` (`cli/attn/src/commands/seed/`), and `attn seed tend <id> --for <name>` to assign a seed to a member.
- `attn agent msg <member> "text"` addresses the member's mailbox. The recipe for that verb is in [inbox.md](inbox.md).
- `attn agent reload <member>` reloads the member's current session in place, under the reload rules in [agent-launch.md](agent-launch.md), with the member's saved model and effort when the member's harness matches. The command refuses a sleeping member, which stays asleep.
- The Chief member: `attn crew set chief --agent <harness> [--model <model>]` configures a Chief that has no harness yet, `attn crew wake chief` wakes the Chief, and `attn crew list --json` marks the Chief `"chief": true`. The Chief's priming includes its guidance (`internal/prompts/content/crew/v2/chief.md`, and `v2/chief-switchover.md` on the first day of a Chief prefilled from a session). The member doc's `first_day` holds the session that was chief until the Chief files its first letter. The member doc also holds the Chief's pending onboarding path (`full` on a fresh install, `tour` when sessions already existed or a session was chief) until `attn onboarding done`.
- Onboarding: `attn onboarding [done|again] [--profile] [--json]` (`cmd/attnd/onboarding.go`, `internal/daemon/onboarding.go`) shows, finishes or restarts the Chief's onboarding. While onboarding is pending, the Chief's priming adds `v2/chief-onboarding.md` (with `-full.md` or `-tour.md`), which sends the Chief to `attn skill --reference onboarding`.
- Prompts: the identity, charter or first day, compaction note, operating model "How you work" (`v2/operating-model.md`), letters, closure and garden in the member's session instructions (`internal/prompts/content/crew/v2/`), the opening that wakes the member (`v2/wake.md`) and the opening a successor gets (`v2/successor.md`). Beside them: the line before the priming that asks a cleared conversation to run `attn crew prime` (`crew/cleared.md`), the sleep request (`crew/sleep-requested.md`, `crew/sleep-away.md`), the apply notices (`crew/switch-requested.md`, `crew/settings-applied.md`) and the heartbeat (`crew/heartbeat.md`), plus the chief's seed assignment (`internal/prompts/content/chief/seed-assignment.md`).
- `attn skill --reference charter`, `attn skill --reference charter` (`internal/prompts/content/skills/attn/references/charter.md`), named "Write or update your crew charter" in the skill's capability index (`internal/prompts/content/skills/attn/SKILL.md`). A member reads the reference on its first day before writing `CHARTER.md`, and when the member adapts the charter to its work. `attn skill` itself is in [agent-guidance.md](agent-guidance.md).

### Triggers

- When an item arrives for an asleep member, attn wakes the member before the ring, with a first prompt that names the items that woke it (`internal/daemon/inbox_wake_cause.go`, `crew/woken-by.md` and `v2/woken-by-charter.md`), within the wake limit of `crew.wake_limit` wakes per `crew.wake_limit_window_seconds` (`internal/daemon/crew_wake.go`, `internal/daemon/inbox_delivery.go`, and rules in `docs/internal/inbox.md`).
- Every 60 seconds the daemon runs a lifecycle tick (`internal/daemon/crew_lifecycle.go`). The tick sends an idle member a cache heartbeat before the member's cache lapses, and asks the member to sleep once the user has been away for `crew.away_seconds`. `crew.heartbeat_enabled` turns off the heartbeat, and `crew.autosleep_enabled` turns off the sleep request.

## Driving it

Preconditions:

- Use the baseline instance. The steps that run `bridge` also need its app. Check that `bridge --launch --wait-ready ping` answers. Run `bridge dismiss_whats_new` to dismiss What's new.
- Make a member home before the daemon starts, since a daemon reads new homes only at start. Run `M="$(./attn instance resolve --field dataDir)/crew/fern" && printf 'export M=%q\n' "$M" >> "$DRIVE_ENV"`, `mkdir -p "$M"` and `printf '# Fern\n\nWait for direction.\n' > "$M/CHARTER.md"`. With a daemon already running, stop it as [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) describes (through `"$(./attn instance resolve --field appDaemon)" daemon stop` once the app is installed), then start it again as the baseline does.
- Make a proof folder with `P=$(mktemp -d /tmp/drive-attn-crew.XXXX) && echo "export P=$P" >> $DRIVE_ENV`.
- Make a member folder with `C=$(mktemp -d /tmp/drive-attn-crew-agent.XXXX) && echo "export C=$C" >> $DRIVE_ENV`, and save this fixture as `$C/.attn-mock-agent.json`:

```json
{"version": 1, "turns": [
  {"includes": "unread items", "actions": [
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["agent", "inbox"]}]},
  {"includes": "woke you", "actions": [
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["agent", "inbox"]}]},
  {"includes": "drive-switch", "actions": [
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["handoff", "-m", "Nothing pending. Check the drive seed."]}]},
  {"includes": "drive-sleep", "actions": [
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["handoff", "-m", "Resting. Nothing pending."]}]},
  {"includes": "You have been woken", "actions": [
    {"type": "reply", "text": "Fern here, awake."}]},
  {"includes": "tell", "actions": [
    {"type": "capture", "name": "to", "pattern": "tell (\\S+)"},
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["agent", "msg", "{{to}}", "hello from Fern"]}]}
]}
```

- Start a sender for mailbox messages with `wsctl add-session --cwd $C --agent shell --label crew-caller --id crew-caller`.

`<fern>` is the terminal of Fern's current session: the `runtime_id` of the tile in `wsctl list` whose `session_id` is the session `./attn crew list` names. Each wake, harness switch and mailbox wake starts a session in a new terminal, so read it again after each. Before each send to Fern, re-read `./attn agent peek fern` until its `state:` line stops reading `working`. A turn that is still running delays the ring.

- **The roster from the shell.** Run `./attn crew list`. Under `MEMBER  STATE  AGENT ...` it lists `Fern  asleep  claude` with its profile and home. Run `./attn crew set fern --cwd $C --agent claude`. It prints `Fern launches in <C> on claude, model ..., effort ...`, `profile: <name>`, `launch desktop: ...` and `awareness dirs: -`.
- **Launch settings from the shell.** Run `./attn crew set fern --awareness-dir $P --launch-desktop 8`. It prints `awareness dirs: <P>` and a `launch desktop:` line for desktop 8. Run `./attn crew set fern --awareness-dir "" --launch-desktop own`: it prints `awareness dirs: -`.
- **Wake from the shell.** Run `./attn crew wake fern`. It prints `Fern is awake in session <id>.` and names `attn agent peek fern`. `./attn crew list` shows `awake` with the session, and `./attn agent peek fern` shows `crew member: this session is Fern today` and `Fern here, awake.` Running the wake again prints `Fern is already awake in session <id> — nothing was launched.`
- **Reload by name.** Run `./attn agent reload fern --source-session crew-caller`: it exits 1 naming Fern's session in a `delegation-chain rule` refusal, since crew-caller is outside that session's delegation chain. Give the fixture a banner the running process does not have yet: `jq '.banner = "Claude Code mock agent (reload by name)"' "$C/.attn-mock-agent.json" > "$C/next.json" && mv "$C/next.json" "$C/.attn-mock-agent.json"`. Run `./attn agent reload fern --source-session <session>` with the session `./attn crew list --json` names for Fern, a self reload. Re-read `./attn agent peek fern` until it shows the `reload by name` banner on the same session.
- **The member's mailbox.** Run `./attn agent msg fern "hello Fern" --source-session crew-caller`. The receipt is `notified: notified Fern (id <message>)`, and peek on fern shows `📨 from crew-cal (crew-caller): hello Fern`.
- **A member's message.** Run `wsctl input --id <fern> --text "tell crew-caller" --enter`. `./attn agent inbox --session crew-caller` prints `📨 from Fern: hello from Fern` and `reply: attn agent msg Fern "..."`: the message names the member, not the member's session, so a reply reaches Fern's current session or wakes Fern.
- **Apply a change in place.** Run `./attn crew set fern --effort high` (any effort the harness accepts), then `./attn crew apply fern`. It prints `Reloaded Fern's session <id> in place on claude with its saved settings; its conversation continues.` `./attn crew list` names the same session, and re-reading `./attn agent peek fern` shows `[attn] This session reloaded with your saved launch settings` once the session reads its inbox.
- **Clear keeps the member awake.** Note the session `./attn crew list` names, then run `wsctl input --id <fern> --text "/clear" --enter`. `./attn crew list --json` shows Fern awake in a different session, in the same terminal `<fern>`, and `./attn agent peek fern` names the new session. `ATTN_TERMINAL_ID=<fern> ./attn crew prime` prints `You are **Fern**` and the charter. `./attn crew prime` from your own shell exits 1 with `this session is not a crew member's`. `./attn crew wake fern` prints `Fern is already awake in session <new id>`.
- **Switch harness into a successor.** Note the session `./attn crew list` names. Run `./attn crew set fern --agent codex`, then `./attn crew apply fern`. It prints `Asked Fern in session <id> to file its letter; its next session starts on codex from it.`, or `Fern's session <id> is busy; it will be asked to file its letter when it can take input, ...`. Re-read `./attn agent peek fern` until it shows `[attn] Your launch settings now run you on codex`. Run `wsctl input --id <fern> --text "drive-switch" --enter`. The old session closes as it files the letter, so do not look for the mock's reply there. `./attn crew list --json` shows Fern awake in a different session on codex, `ls "$M/handoffs"` lists one letter with its session record, `<letter>.json`, beside it, and `./attn agent peek fern` names the new session. The successor's opening matches no fixture turn, so it stays silent. Run `./attn crew set fern --agent claude` so later wakes run on claude again.
- **Sleep on request.** Read `<fern>` again for the new session. Run `./attn crew sleep fern`. It prints ``Asked Fern in session <id> to write its handoff and file it with `attn handoff`.``, or `Sleep request for Fern is queued in session <id> — ...` while the session is not at a prompt. The ring reaches the session, which reads its inbox: re-read `./attn agent peek fern` until it shows `[attn] The user is asking you to close your session and sleep.` Run `wsctl input --id <fern> --text "drive-sleep" --enter`. The session closes as it files the letter. `./attn crew list` shows `asleep`, and `ls "$M/handoffs"` lists a second letter and its `.json`. `./attn agent reload fern --source-session crew-caller` now exits 1 with `crew member Fern is asleep; reload does not wake members`, and Fern stays asleep.
- **Wake from the mailbox.** With Fern asleep, run `./attn agent msg fern "wake for this" --source-session crew-caller`. The receipt starts `queued: woke Fern in session <id>; notification queued until it reaches a safe prompt`. Read `<fern>` again. The new session's first prompt names what woke it: `Updates woke you; the user did not open this session:` and `- A message from crew-caller.`, without the message's text (`grep -h "woke you" $C/.attn-mock-agent/*.jsonl` finds it). The session reads its inbox at once: re-read `./attn agent peek fern` until it shows `📨 from crew-cal (crew-caller): wake for this`.
- **The panel from the palette.** Open the palette as the README says, type `Manage crew` and press Enter. `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"[data-testid=\"crew-panel\"]","timeoutMs":5000}'` matches. Click `[data-testid="crew-roster-fern"]` natively. On the launch tab, `[data-testid="crew-sleep"]` reads `Sleep` while Fern is awake. Click the button natively, and `./attn crew list` shows `awake` with a new session.

Click `[data-testid="crew-tab-charter"]` natively, then click `[data-testid="crew-charter-editor"]` natively. Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"ArrowDown","modifiers":["command"]}'`, `bridge native_key '{"key":"Enter"}'` and `bridge native_text '{"text":"Keep the drive notes short."}'`. `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"[data-testid=\"crew-charter-status\"]","textIncludes":"Saved","timeoutMs":5000}'` matches, and `cat "$M/CHARTER.md"` ends with the new line. - **Find, apply, seeds and close in the panel.** With Fern awake, run `./attn crew set fern --effort medium`. Open "Manage crew" from the palette, click `[data-testid="crew-roster-fern"]` natively, then `[data-testid="crew-apply"]`: re-reading `./attn agent peek fern` shows `[attn] This session reloaded with your saved launch settings`. - **Wake with another harness.** Put Fern to sleep as above, then run `./attn crew wake fern --agent codex`. Read `<fern>` again. `./attn agent peek fern` shows `session <id> (codex) — Fern` and `Fern here, awake.` The session launched with Codex memory off: `grep -l "memories.generate_memories=false" $C/.attn-mock-agent/*.jsonl` lists its launch record.
- **The letter's flags.** Run `./attn crew create Ivy`, `./attn crew set Ivy --cwd $C --agent claude` and `./attn crew wake Ivy`, with `<ivy>` the session `./attn crew list --json` names. `./attn handoff --retry --session <ivy>` exits 1 with `Ivy's day has filed no letter yet, so there is no turnover to retry`. `printf 'Letter from stdin.' | ./attn handoff -m - --session <ivy> --json` prints `"outcome": "sleep"` and the letter's `path`, whose file holds `Letter from stdin.`. `./attn crew list` shows Ivy asleep. `./attn handoff --sleep -m x --session <ivy>` exits 2 with `flag provided but not defined: -sleep`. Retire Ivy.
- **Create, retire and restore.** Run `./attn crew create Keel`. It prints `Keel joined profile <name> with key member:keel.` and its home, and `cat "<home>/CHARTER.md"` is empty. `./attn crew create keel` exits 1 with `Keel already exists`. - **The Chief member.** The Chief exists from the daemon's first start. `./attn crew list --json` has a member `Chief` with `"chief": true` and no `binding_session`. ## Contexts

- **Durability across restarts**: charters and letters are files in the member home, and the roster, launch settings and bindings are the daemon's records. A daemon crash during a clear still leaves the member awake in the new session. An awake member's session comes back like any session, and unread mailbox items stay for the member's next session.
- **Linux**: `attn crew` and `attn handoff` work the same where the daemon and CLI run on Linux. The crew has no shortcut. The palette reaches the crew.
- **Outposts**: a member's sessions start on the home's machine. `attn crew set` has no host.
- **Profiles**: every member belongs to one profile and wakes only there. Names resolve only in the caller's profile (the calling session's, else `--profile`, else the app's selected profile). A member of another profile answers as if the member did not exist, including a message sent to that member. Two profiles can each have a Bob. Each profile has one Chief.
- **Performance and idle cost**: the sidebar's wake sun animates to a held frame when armed and stops. A glow that keeps moving is a repaint defect. Each heartbeat is a turn typed into an idle member's session before the member's cache lapses. The cache lasts 3600 seconds for Claude and 1800 for Codex unless you set `crew.cache_ttl_seconds`, and the heartbeat comes `crew.heartbeat_lead_seconds` earlier.
- **Harnesses**: `attn crew set --agent` picks claude, codex or an installed plugin driver, and `attn crew wake --agent` overrides that choice for one session. Member sessions launch with harness memory off: Claude Code with `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_AUTO_MEMORY=1`, Codex with `features.memories=false` and `memories.generate_memories=false` on its thread. Claude Code gets "How you work" in its appended system instructions, and Codex, plain or shared, gets it in its developer instructions.
- **Keyboard and native input**: the palette opens the panel, Escape closes it, and the wake sun is a button that Enter or Space arms and then confirms.
- **Compatibility across versions**: crew memory was removed. attn records the session that filed a letter, so attn keeps that session's transcript. The store's crew migrations carry older member records forward. `attn handoff --sleep` and `--nap` are retired and exit 2 with `flag provided but not defined: -sleep` or `-nap`. A member session an earlier build started was told to file with `attn handoff --sleep` to sleep. Plain `attn handoff` now also sleeps the member. A session started by an earlier build therefore sleeps on plain `attn handoff` even when its instructions promised a successor while the user is present.
- **Flow**: in desktop flow, the sidebar lists asleep members above the desktops. In queue flow, the queue sidebar has a crew block with awake and asleep members, and "Put the crew in the queue" lets a member that is owed a turn wait in the queue, the Chief included.

## Gotchas

- `attn crew create` adds a member at once. The daemon adds a home folder made by hand to the roster only at start, when it finds `<key>/CHARTER.md` in the instance's crew folder. A home folder made while the daemon runs stays invisible until the next start. Keys are lowercase letters, digits and `-`, starting with a letter.
- The app's roster and `attn crew list` leave out retired members. Only `attn crew list --all` shows them. A retired member's awake session keeps running until the session files its letter, and that letter always ends with the member asleep, even with a harness switch pending.
- Both wake openings mention the member's letter, so a fixture turn that matches `letter` fires at wake. Match on words the opening does not carry, as the fixture above does.
- The wake opening starts `You have been woken. Get up to speed` and the successor's starts `Your previous session filed the letter above`. Match words the opening carries, such as `You have been woken`, as the fixture's wake turn does.
- A sleep request is an inbox notice: the member reads it with `attn agent inbox` after the 📬 ring, and the 30 seconds of quiet after any `wsctl input` to that terminal apply. Only the member's own `attn handoff` ends the session. `attn crew sleep` and the panel's Sleep only send the request.
- `attn handoff` leaves the member asleep even while the user is at the machine. Only a letter that `attn crew apply` asked for after a harness change starts a successor, so a run that expects one applies a harness change first. `attn handoff --sleep` and `--nap` exit 2 with `flag provided but not defined`. The mock agent shows that error as a `mock agent error:` block while the session stays awake.
- Inbox wakes count against `crew.wake_limit`. A run that wakes a member by message many times hits it, and the receipt names the setting. Wakes from the sidebar or `attn crew wake` do not count.
- A seed sent to the Chief and mail to `chief` go to the Chief member and wake the Chief like any member, unless the Chief has no harness yet.
- The panel's Wake stays disabled until the launch settings are saved.
- Wake selects the member's session and closes Manage crew. Reopen the panel before looking for the member's charter or letters. The closed panel's root remains in the DOM with zero-size bounds, so the root's presence alone does not show that the panel is open.
- The CLI and the panel still use older words for a member's session and letters (the "Handoffs" tab, `attn handoff`, the sleep request's "handoff"). Match the literal text shown here. The glossary lists those words as debt, not as a failure of the run.
- Working but wrong: `Asked Fern ...`, `Sleep request for Fern is queued ...` and `Fern's session <id> is busy; it will be asked ...` only say the request was recorded. Fern is asleep when `./attn crew list` says so. Likewise `awake` says a session launched, not that the member read its charter: prove a wake by the member's reply in `./attn agent peek fern`.
