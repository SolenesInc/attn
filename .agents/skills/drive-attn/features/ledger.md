# Ledger

Every session, live and closed, like a chat history: resume a closed one as
itself, keep or forget its conversation. Each profile has one ledger. A
session is attn's record of one conversation, and closing it keeps its row
with who closed it and why. People open the ledger to filter it, focus a live
session, or bring a closed one back in a tile under its own id; its
Conversations list keeps attn's own copies of Claude conversations. Agents
list and read sessions, rename their own, read transcripts and resume sessions
from the CLI. When a harness moves to another conversation, its terminal shows
that conversation's session and the one it left goes to the ledger.

## Sub-features

- `ledger-list`: Live, Closed or All, newest first, filtered by time, profile and repository, in pages.
- `ledger-row`: one session with its harness, the outpost it ran on, place, usage, who closed it and why, and whether it can come back.
- `resume`: bring a closed or stopped session back under its own id, on its last or current desktop; an action that recreates a worktree or fetches a branch runs only when named.
- `rename`: the name people read in the sidebar and the ledger.
- `transcript`: a session's conversation as timestamped, redacted events.
- `session-question`: ask a question of a Codex session's conversation and get exact excerpts back.
- `session-tree`: every live session under the session that dispatched it.
- `conversation-move`: /clear, /new, /resume or Codex /agents move a terminal to that conversation's session.
- `hidden-sessions`: live over a link with no tile showing them, marked hidden.
- `kept-conversations`: attn's own copy of a Claude conversation its harness would delete: keep it forever, release it, or forget it.

## How to get to it

### People

- ⌘⇧L (`sessions.open`, `app/src/shortcuts/registry.ts`) opens the ledger, the dialog "Sessions, worktrees and conversations", on the Sessions list. The palette's `sessions` ("Open the sessions list", `app/src/application/useAppCommands.tsx`) and the sidebar's "Open Ledger (⌘⇧L)" button (`app/src/application/useAppSidebarActions.tsx`) do the same.
- In the ledger (`app/src/components/ledger/LedgerSurface.tsx`): the lists Sessions, Worktrees and Conversations, with `]` moving to the next one; Worktrees belongs to [worktrees.md](worktrees.md). Sessions (`app/src/components/ledger/SessionsTab.tsx`) has the Live, Closed and All switch, a query field that takes tokens such as `profile:` and `repo:`, and on each row Focus for a live session, "Reopen" for a closed one, Show worktree, the seed it tends, and Keep conversation or Unkeep for a Claude session; a row that ran on an outpost names it and offers neither Show worktree nor Keep conversation. Conversations (`app/src/components/ledger/ConversationsTab.tsx`) takes Enter to keep or unkeep a row and `2` to ask to Forget it; "Forget…" is not offered while an open seed keeps the copy. A copy that crew letters keep lists them as `Crew letter: <Member> · <date>`, and its confirmation adds `These letters stay, but will no longer point to this transcript: <Member> · <date>.`; "Forget now" then unlinks the letters and deletes the copy in one step.
- Palette `rename-active-session` ("Rename this session…") opens the "Rename session" dialog for the agent on screen; the sidebar row's actions menu has "Rename session" (`app/src/components/SessionActionsPopover.tsx`).
- A stopped tile's Resume is in [agent-launch.md](agent-launch.md).
- Their own shell: the verbs below. Outside attn, `--profile <id>` picks the profile.

### Agents

- `attn session` (`cmd/attn/session_instructions.go` dispatches it; the ledger verbs are in `cmd/attn/session_ledger.go`, `cmd/attn/session_reopen.go` and `cmd/attn/session_tree.go`): `list` with `--closed`, `--all`, `--tree`, `--reopen`, `--limit`, `--before`, `--repository`, `--last`, `--since`, `--until`, `--profile` and `--json`; `show <id>`; `transcript <id>` with `--after`, `--follow` and `--json`; `instructions <id> --question <text>` (Codex sessions only; a background model answers, with its prompts in `internal/prompts/content/session-instructions/`); `rename <name> [--session <id>]`; `reopen <id> [--action <name>]`. `priority`, `snooze` and `unsnooze` belong to [queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md).
- `attn conversation list [--all] [--json]`, `keep`, `unkeep` and `forget <session-or-conversation-id> [--unlink-crew-letters]` (`cmd/attn/conversation.go`), taking `session:<id>` or `conversation:<id>` when an id is ambiguous. `forget` refuses a copy that crew letters keep and names each letter as `<member> <filename>`. `--unlink-crew-letters` consents: attn removes each letter's session record (the `.json` beside the letter in the member home's `handoffs/`), leaves the letters unchanged, deletes its copy and prints `letters that lost their transcript (letters are unchanged):` with one `<member> <filename>` line per letter. Open seeds block `forget` with or without the flag: harvest or wither them first.
- `attn list` prints the live sessions and the profiles as JSON (`cmd/attn/main.go`).
- Prompts: the garden contract tells agents to rename their session when the work drifts (`internal/prompts/content/garden.md`); the attn skill's reference on other sessions names `session list --tree` and `attn list --json` (`internal/prompts/content/skills/attn/references/converse-and-observe.md`).

### Triggers

- When a harness moves to another conversation, with /clear, /new, /resume or Codex /agents, the terminal shows that conversation's session. A Claude session left behind closes into the ledger; under shared Codex it stays open and hidden (`internal/daemon/monitor_conversation.go`, `internal/daemon/agent_conversation.go`).
- A keep pass decides which Claude conversations attn copies and when unpinned copies are deleted (`internal/daemon/conversation_keep.go`).

## Contexts

- **Durability across restarts**: the ledger is the daemon's record. Closed rows, their reasons, names and kept conversations survive every restart. A session a machine restart stopped comes back `recoverable` and resumes on request.
- **Linux**: ⌘⇧L is Ctrl+Alt+L (`LINUX_SHORTCUTS`). The `attn session` and `attn conversation` verbs work the same where the daemon runs.
- **Outposts**: `attn session instructions` from an outpost is answered by the home, which holds the ledger. A live or closed row shows the outpost its session ran on by name, or by its host id once that outpost is removed, and offers neither Show worktree nor Keep conversation, since an outpost conversation has no kept copy at the home. "Reopen" or `attn session reopen <id>` resumes a closed outpost session on that same outpost, with its conversation and cost continuing; when its worktree was deleted, the `recreate_worktree_and_reopen` and `fetch_recreate_and_reopen` actions recreate it there, the second fetching the branch first. Resume refuses and the row stays closed when the outpost is unreachable or removed or Outposts is off (the refusal names the host), when the folder no longer exists there, and when the harness no longer has the conversation (`conversation <id> is no longer in codex's storage`).
- **Profiles**: each profile has its own ledger, and a session belongs to one profile for life. Resume lands in the session's own profile; a session whose profile was deleted cannot come back. Inside attn the session's profile scopes `session list --tree`; outside, `--profile` narrows it. The glossary still lists checking that a request with no profile does not list every profile.
- **Performance and idle cost**: the ledger reads in pages. The CLI ends a page with `showing N, M omitted, paginate with --before <id>`, and the dialog offers older rows on request.
- **Harnesses**: keeping a copy is for Claude Code conversations only, so Keep conversation shows on Claude rows that ran at the home. /clear moves Claude Code, /new moves Codex, /resume both, and Codex /agents under shared Codex. Under shared Codex the session left behind stays live and hidden instead of closing ([shared-codex.md](shared-codex.md)). Two Claude Code or Pi tiles on one conversation are two sessions, and both warn ([harnesses.md](harnesses.md)).
- **Keyboard and native input**: ⌘⇧L opens the ledger, `]` moves across its lists, arrows walk rows, Enter runs a row's first action, `2` asks to forget a kept conversation, and Escape closes. In the Forget confirmation, Enter is Cancel and `2` is "Forget now".
- **Compatibility across versions**: a later version reads today's sessions and kept conversations through the store's migrations. `attn conversation forget` against a daemon from before `--unlink-crew-letters` deletes the copy, then exits 1 with `daemon returned no conversation forget result`; restart the daemon on the CLI's build before driving `forget`. The CLI's stale-daemon warning shows only when the version numbers differ, so two builds of the same version give none.

- A fixture with `"resumable": true` writes the mock's conversation where the harness keeps its own: `.claude/projects` or `.codex/sessions` under `ATTN_TOOL_HOME`, which the baseline sets to a scratch folder.
