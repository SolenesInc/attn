# Ledger

Every session, live and closed, like a chat history: resume a closed session
under its own id, and keep or forget its conversation. Each profile has one ledger. A
session is attn's record of one conversation. When a session closes, attn
keeps its row with who closed it and why. People open the ledger to filter it,
focus a live session, or bring a closed one back in a tile under its own id.
The ledger's Conversations list holds attn's own copies of Claude
conversations. Agents list and read sessions, rename their own, read
transcripts and resume sessions from the CLI. When a harness moves a terminal
to another conversation, the terminal shows that conversation's session. The
session the terminal left closes into the ledger, except under shared Codex, where it
stays open and hidden.

## Sub-features

- `ledger-list`: Live, Closed or All, newest first, filtered by time, profile and repository, in pages.
- `ledger-row`: one session with its harness, the outpost it ran on, place, usage, who closed it and why, and whether it can come back.
- `resume`: bring a closed or stopped session back under its own id, on its last or current desktop. An action that recreates a worktree or fetches a branch runs only when you name it.
- `rename`: the name people read in the sidebar and the ledger.
- `transcript`: a session's conversation as timestamped, redacted events.
- `session-question`: ask a question of a Codex session's conversation and get exact excerpts back.
- `session-tree`: every live session under the session that dispatched it.
- `conversation-move`: /clear, /new, /resume or Codex /agents move a terminal to that conversation's session.
- `hidden-sessions`: live over a link with no tile showing them, marked hidden.
- `kept-conversations`: attn's own copy of a Claude conversation its harness would delete: keep the copy forever, release it, or forget it.

## How to get to it

### People

- ⌘⇧L (`sessions.open`, `app/src/shortcuts/registry.ts`) opens the ledger, the dialog "Sessions, worktrees and conversations", on the Sessions list. The palette's `sessions` ("Open the sessions list", `app/src/application/useAppCommands.tsx`) and the sidebar's "Open Ledger (⌘⇧L)" button (`app/src/application/useAppSidebarActions.tsx`) do the same.
- In the ledger (`app/src/components/ledger/LedgerSurface.tsx`), the lists are Sessions, Worktrees and Conversations. Press `]` to move to the next list. For Worktrees, see [worktrees.md](worktrees.md). Sessions (`app/src/components/ledger/SessionsTab.tsx`) has the Live, Closed and All switch, a query field that takes tokens such as `profile:` and `repo:`, and on each row Focus for a live session, "Reopen" for a closed one, Show worktree, the seed the session tends, and Keep conversation or Unkeep for a Claude session. A row for a session that ran on an outpost names the outpost and offers neither Show worktree nor Keep conversation. Conversations (`app/src/components/ledger/ConversationsTab.tsx`) takes Enter to keep or unkeep a row and `2` to ask to Forget it. "Forget…" is not offered while an open seed keeps the copy. A copy that crew letters keep lists the letters as `Crew letter: <Member> · <date>`, and the confirmation adds `These letters stay, but will no longer point to this transcript: <Member> · <date>.` "Forget now" then unlinks the letters and deletes the copy in one step.
- Palette `rename-active-session` ("Rename this session…") opens the "Rename session" dialog for the agent on screen. The sidebar row's actions menu has "Rename session" (`app/src/components/SessionActionsPopover.tsx`).
- A stopped tile's Resume is in [agent-launch.md](agent-launch.md).
- Their own shell: the verbs below. Outside attn, `--profile <id>` picks the profile.

### Agents

- `attn session` (`cmd/attn/session_instructions.go` dispatches it, and the ledger verbs are in `cmd/attn/session_ledger.go`, `cmd/attn/session_reopen.go` and `cmd/attn/session_tree.go`): `list` with `--closed`, `--all`, `--tree`, `--reopen`, `--limit`, `--before`, `--repository`, `--last`, `--since`, `--until`, `--profile` and `--json`. `show <id>`. `transcript <id>` with `--after`, `--follow` and `--json`. `instructions <id> --question <text>` works on Codex sessions only, and a background model answers the question, with the model's prompts in `internal/prompts/content/session-instructions/`. `rename <name> [--session <id>]`. `reopen <id> [--action <name>]`. `priority`, `snooze` and `unsnooze` belong to [queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md).
- `attn conversation list [--all] [--json]`, `keep`, `unkeep` and `forget <session-or-conversation-id> [--unlink-crew-letters]` (`cmd/attn/conversation.go`). These verbs take `session:<id>` or `conversation:<id>` when an id is ambiguous. `forget` refuses a copy that crew letters keep and names each letter as `<member> <filename>`. With `--unlink-crew-letters`, you consent to unlinking. attn then removes each letter's session record (the `.json` beside the letter in the member home's `handoffs/`), leaves the letters unchanged, deletes the copy and prints `letters that lost their transcript (letters are unchanged):` with one `<member> <filename>` line per letter. Open seeds block `forget` with or without the flag. Harvest or wither them first.
- `attn list` prints the live sessions and the profiles as JSON (`cmd/attn/main.go`).
- Prompts: the garden contract tells agents to rename their session when the work drifts (`internal/prompts/content/garden.md`). The attn skill's reference on other sessions names `session list --tree` and `attn list --json` (`internal/prompts/content/skills/attn/references/converse-and-observe.md`).

### Triggers

- When a harness moves to another conversation, with /clear, /new, /resume or Codex /agents, the terminal shows that conversation's session. A Claude session left behind closes into the ledger. Under shared Codex, the session left behind stays open and hidden (`internal/daemon/monitor_conversation.go`, `internal/daemon/agent_conversation.go`).
- The daemon runs a keep pass that chooses which Claude conversations attn copies and when attn deletes unpinned copies (`internal/daemon/conversation_keep.go`).

## Contexts

- **Durability across restarts**: the ledger is the daemon's record. Closed rows, their reasons, names and kept conversations survive every restart. An agent a machine restart stopped resumes on its own; a shell stays `recoverable` and starts again on request.
- **Linux**: ⌘⇧L is Ctrl+Alt+L (`LINUX_SHORTCUTS`). The `attn session` and `attn conversation` verbs work the same where the daemon runs.
- **Outposts**: the home, which holds the ledger, answers `attn session instructions` from an outpost. A live or closed row shows the outpost its session ran on by name, or by its host id once that outpost is removed, and offers neither Show worktree nor Keep conversation, since an outpost conversation has no kept copy at the home. "Reopen" or `attn session reopen <id>` resumes a closed outpost session on that same outpost, and the session's conversation and cost continue. When the session's worktree was deleted, the `recreate_worktree_and_reopen` and `fetch_recreate_and_reopen` actions recreate the worktree there, and the second action fetches the branch first. Resume refuses and the row stays closed in three cases. In the first, the outpost is unreachable, the outpost is removed, or Outposts is off, and the refusal names the host. In the second, the folder no longer exists there. In the third, the harness no longer has the conversation (`conversation <id> is no longer in codex's storage`).
- **Profiles**: each profile has its own ledger, and a session belongs to one profile for life. Resume lands in the session's own profile. A session whose profile was deleted cannot come back. Inside attn, the session's profile scopes `session list --tree`. Outside attn, `--profile` narrows the list. The glossary still lists a to-do: check whether the ledger lists every profile when a request has no profile.
- **Performance and idle cost**: attn loads the ledger in pages. The CLI ends a page with `showing N, M omitted, paginate with --before <id>`, and the dialog offers older rows on request.
- **Harnesses**: attn keeps copies of Claude Code conversations only, so Keep conversation shows on Claude rows that ran at the home. In Claude Code, /clear and /resume move a terminal to another conversation. In Codex, /new and /resume do, and so does /agents under shared Codex. Under shared Codex the session left behind stays live and hidden instead of closing ([shared-codex.md](shared-codex.md)). Two Claude Code tiles on one conversation are two sessions, and both warn ([harnesses.md](harnesses.md)).
- **Keyboard and native input**: ⌘⇧L opens the ledger, `]` moves across its lists, arrows walk rows, Enter runs a row's first action, `2` asks to forget a kept conversation, and Escape closes. In the Forget confirmation, Enter is Cancel and `2` is "Forget now".
- **Compatibility across versions**: a later version reads today's sessions and kept conversations through the store's migrations. `attn conversation forget` against a daemon from before `--unlink-crew-letters` deletes the copy, then exits 1 with `daemon returned no conversation forget result`. Restart the daemon on the CLI's build before driving `forget`. The CLI's stale-daemon warning shows only when the version numbers differ, so two builds of the same version give no warning.

## Gotchas

- An answer from `session instructions` needs a real available Codex transcript as well as the background model. The Codex branch of **A question for a session** failed on the baseline at `b292a5aa5` and again on `drive-1`: after `hello`, `./attn session instructions ledger-codex --question "what did the user ask?"` exited 1 with `The target transcript is unavailable`, before the expected model-unavailable refusal. The mock's Codex transcript does not satisfy the command's required conversation format, so the mock cannot reach the model-refusal check or a successful answer as written. Keep the expected step and report this failed precondition.

- The ledger fills the app window and hides the sidebar's instance marker. A screenshot of the ledger cannot satisfy the README's visible-sidebar-marker rule. Keep the routed bridge read and a native window capture naming the instance in the title bar. Record the marker gap in the run report.

- A fixture with `"resumable": true` writes the mock's conversation where the harness keeps its own: `.claude/projects` or `.codex/sessions` under `ATTN_TOOL_HOME`, which the baseline sets to a scratch folder. A daemon started without `ATTN_TOOL_HOME` writes into your real `~/.claude` or `~/.codex`. If that happens, stop the daemon and launch it again in the instance shell. Without `resumable`, the conversation lives in the agent's folder and resume can find nothing to bring back.
- `attn list` takes no flags and never prints help: `attn list --help` lists every session as JSON. From a shell with no instance selected it reads production.
- `session rename` and `agent close` act as the calling session. From a plain shell they need `--session` or `--source-session`. Drive the agent's entrypoint through the mock.
- `session show` and `session list` pad their labels to the widest row. Match `state      closed` as the label and its value, with any spacing.
- `session instructions` sounds like it prints the launch instructions attn gives a session ([agent-guidance.md](agent-guidance.md)). It does not. It asks a model about another session's conversation and never prints what attn told that session.
- After `/clear`, wait for the new session in `./attn agent list` before reading the closed row.
- A live session's conversation is copied only after its transcript has been quiet for a day. `conversation keep` on a live session prints `0 kept` with `1 pending` and `forget` refuses with `attn has no live copy of conversation ... to forget`. Keep a closed session, or append `export ATTN_CONVERSATION_KEEP_QUIET=0s` to the env file before the daemon starts.
- An open seed that keeps the copy blocks `forget` with `... is kept by open seeds: ...; harvest or wither them before forgetting`, with or without `--unlink-crew-letters`. When removing a letter's session record or attn's copy fails partway, the error adds `unlinked crew letters: <letters, or none>; letters are unchanged`, and while attn's copy remains it ends with the exact `attn conversation forget conversation:<id> --unlink-crew-letters` to rerun. Working but wrong: after a consented Forget, the row still shows `Crew letter:`, or the letter differs from before. The correct result is that the row drops its letters and the letter stays as it was.
- The baseline has no outpost to resume on ([outposts.md](outposts.md)), so resume on an outpost cannot be driven there. `scripts/verify-outpost-ledger-resume.sh` and `cmd/attn/session_reopen_outpost_stack_test.go` prove it.
- `sessions_set_filter` and `sessions_row_action` click through the DOM. Use them to set up. Prove a person's way in with native clicks or keys.
- "Show worktree" opens the Worktrees list, which covers only repositories attn made a worktree in ([worktrees.md](worktrees.md)). A worktree made by hand shows "No worktrees tracked yet" there.
- Under shared Codex, `/agents` and `/resume` take the mock's thread id, `mock-<uuid>`. Neither `session show` nor `conversation list` prints that id. [shared-codex.md](shared-codex.md)'s "Switch back by id" says where to read it.
- The plain Codex mock's transcript holds only its first reply, without the user's prompts or later turns, so `session transcript --follow` on a Codex mock never shows a new turn.
- `session list --last` takes only its presets, `30d`, `7d`, `today` and `yesterday`. `--last 1h` exits 2 naming them. `--since` and `--until` leave out a live session that has never been seen, such as one a `/clear` just started.
- Working but wrong: the row action and `attn session reopen` say Reopen, and the palette describes the ledger "with reopen", where the glossary says resume. The palette says "Open the sessions list" and the sidebar "Open Ledger" for the same dialog.
