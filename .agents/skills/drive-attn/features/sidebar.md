# Sidebar

The sidebar is the window's list of what the user works with. Its shape depends
on the profile's flow. In desktop flow, the sidebar is the desktop sidebar. It
shows each desktop with its agents and tiles, and collapses to a thin column of
desktop chips. In queue flow, the sidebar is the queue sidebar. It shows Home,
the crew, the agents that are owed a turn, the rest of the agents, automation
runs and desktop chips, and collapses to the queue bar across the top. The
title bar holds the profile, Commands, the flow switch and the buttons for the
garden, the notebook, the ledger, automations, notifications and asks, and the
queue bar's contents too. The sidebar has no header.

## Sub-features

- `desktop-sidebar`: desktop flow, with Crew pills (`sidebar.crew`), Desktops (active desktops and the current desktop's well, with non-current empty desktops folded into dashed pills at `sidebar.empty_desktops`), and Automations (`sidebar.automations.section`) for unslotted automation runs and their delegates.
- `collapsed-rail`: the desktop sidebar collapsed to a column with Home, user desktop chips, and a single ⚡ Automations chip (`rail.automations`) when unslotted automation runs exist.
- `queue-sidebar`: queue flow, with Home, compact Crew pills (`sidebar.crew`), the tonal waiting well (`queue.waiting-well`) with the oldest turns, inline counts on `queue.agents-toggle`, flat Automations (`queue.runs`), and filtered desktop chips (`queue.desktops` with `overview ⌘G` in `queue.desktop-overview`).
- `queue-bar`: the queue sidebar collapsed into a strip: the waiting pill and its peek, asks, runs and desktop chips. The strip is part of the title bar, without its own profile and tools or the asks chip, and the shelf sits inside it ([desktops.md](desktops.md)).
- `agent-list`: every agent of the profile under the waiting card, with a filter.
- `collapse`: hide the sidebar down to the rail or the bar, and bring it back.
- `title-bar`: one 40px toolbar in the window's title bar. Left, beside the traffic lights: New, Commands, the flow switch, the sidebar toggle, notifications. Middle: the shelf, or in queue flow with the sidebar collapsed the queue bar's waiting pill, runs and desktop chips around it. Right: garden, notebook, ledger, automations, asks, the instance marker and the profile pill. The rail keeps Home and desktop chips.
- `resize`: drag the open sidebar's right edge. The tile area keeps its width during the drag and fits once on release. A double-click clears the width.
- `width-steps`: keys and palette commands move the open sidebar by 40px and name a limit they can't pass. The limits are 200px, 600px and the window less 480px for tiles.
- `width-setting`: the width in Settings → Appearance and in `sidebar_width`, with a way back to the default of 240px.
- `session-rows`: one row per agent with its state, harness mark and actions menu. A clipped name shows in full on hover. Clicking the row of an off-screen tile slides the row to show the tile and keeps its order, and hovering the row opens its peek panel ([desktops.md](desktops.md)). The row a delegation chain or pull requests popover opened from is outlined, and the row of the agent the chain picks is lit ([delegation.md](delegation.md)).

## How to get to it

### People

- Keys (`native/crates/attn-app/src/keymap.rs`): ⌘B (`session.toggleSidebar`) collapses and expands the sidebar. ⌘⇧A (`sidebar.agentList`) opens and closes the agent list. Where the queue sidebar is not showing, ⌘⇧A opens the agent palette instead (`native/crates/attn-app/src/sidebar/mod.rs`). ⌥⌘] (`sidebar.wider`) and ⌥⌘[ (`sidebar.narrower`) step the width. They follow the physical keys on every layout ([shortcuts.md](shortcuts.md)).
- Palette (`native/crates/attn-app/src/palette/commands.rs`): `toggle-sidebar` ("Hide the sidebar" or "Show the sidebar"), and `sidebar-wider` ("Widen the sidebar"), `sidebar-narrower` ("Narrow the sidebar") and `sidebar-reset-width` ("Reset sidebar width").
- Title bar (`native/crates/attn-app/src/shell.rs`, `native/crates/attn-app/src/title_bar.rs`): `titlebar.left` holds `titlebar.new` ("New agent"), `titlebar.commands` ("Commands"), `titlebar.flow`, `titlebar.sidebar` ("Collapse sidebar", or "Show sidebar" when collapsed) and the bell, `titlebar.notifications` (`native/crates/attn-app/src/notifications/view.rs`). `titlebar.right` holds `titlebar.garden`, `titlebar.notebook`, `titlebar.ledger`, `titlebar.automations`, `titlebar.asks` (`native/crates/attn-app/src/asks/view.rs`), the instance marker and the profile pill, `titlebar.profile` (`native/crates/attn-app/src/profile_switcher.rs`). Its panel buttons are named by what they do, such as "Show the garden" and "Show Notifications". What each button opens is described in that button's feature file. `titlebar.middle` holds the shelf, or in queue flow with the sidebar collapsed the queue bar.
- Desktop sidebar and rail (`native/crates/attn-app/src/sidebar/mod.rs`, `native/crates/attn-app/src/sidebar/chips.rs`): compact Crew pills (`sidebar.crew.<member>`, and `chief-slot` for the Chief before it has a session), rows `sidebar.session.<session>`, named "Open <name>", with the actions button `sidebar.session.<session>.actions`, desktop groups `sidebar.desktop.<desktop>` (active and current desktops stacked, non-current empty user desktops folded as dashed pills in `sidebar.empty_desktops`), and unslotted automation runs and their delegates under `sidebar.automations.section` (finished rows folded under `sidebar.automations.finished`). Dragging a stacked desktop row reorders the desktops (`native/crates/attn-app/src/sidebar/reorder.rs`). The rail is `rail`, with `rail.home`, a `rail.desktop.<number>` chip per user desktop (omitting unslotted automation desktops holding only runs and their delegates, empty ephemeral launch desktops, and non-current empty crew launch desktops), and a single `rail.automations` ⚡ chip when those automation desktops exist. A row's delegation chain chip stays on a dispatcher whose only delegate closed on standby, and its card shows that delegate as a dormant row; the sidebar itself lists no row for it ([delegation.md](delegation.md)).
- The edge, `sidebar.resize` (`native/crates/attn-app/src/sidebar/width.rs`): drag it to change the width, press Escape mid-drag to cancel, and double-click it to clear the width. `sidebar.width-label` shows the width after a key step, and names a limit at one.
- Settings → Appearance (`native/crates/attn-app/src/settings/appearance.rs`): "Sidebar width (pixels)", `settings.setting.sidebar_width`. "Use default", `settings.sidebar-width.default`, shows while the width differs from 240 and clears the setting. A refused value shows the daemon's reason beside the field.
- Queue sidebar and bar (`native/crates/attn-app/src/sidebar/queue_view.rs`, `native/crates/attn-app/src/sidebar/queue.rs`, `native/crates/attn-app/src/sidebar/bar_peek.rs`): compact Crew pills (`sidebar.crew`), tonal waiting well (`queue.waiting-well`), `queue.agents-toggle` with inline `queue.agents-counts`, the filter "Filter agents", flat Automations (`queue.runs`, finished runs under `sidebar.automations.finished`), and filtered desktop chips (`queue.desktops`, `queue.desktop-overview`). A letter typed with the list open filters it, and Escape clears the filter, then closes the list. On the bar, `titlebar.sidebar` expands it, and the waiting pill `queue-bar.pill` opens the agent palette. Hovering the pill shows a peek of the agents, `queue-bar.peek`.
- Every row's actions menu: "Mark priority", "Rename session", "Move to desktop…", "Reload session" and "Close session" (`native/crates/attn-app/src/sidebar/session_menu.rs`). See [queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md), [ledger.md](ledger.md), [crew.md](crew.md), [desktops.md](desktops.md) and [agent-launch.md](agent-launch.md) for what each does.
- Driven in other files: ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ step through what the sidebar lists, desktops in desktop flow ([desktops.md](desktops.md)) and the queue in queue flow ([queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md)). People switch, rename, close and drag between desktops here ([desktops.md](desktops.md)). The flow button changes the shape ([queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md)). The dock of shortcut chips sits at the foot of the desktop sidebar ([shortcuts.md](shortcuts.md)). See [harnesses.md](harnesses.md) for the harness logos on each row, and see [instances.md](instances.md) for the instance marker in the title bar.
- CLI: `attn settings set sidebar_width <200-600>` moves the width live, and an empty value clears it.
- Where people see what agents do: each row's state dot, harness mark, priority flag and turn age, the waiting count, and the waiting dot on desktop chips.

### Agents

None. Agents change what the sidebar shows without driving it: `attn session rename` sets the name a row shows ([ledger.md](ledger.md)), and `attn callout` can point at the `sidebar`, `home`, `commands`, `garden`, `desktops`, `queue` and `crew` anchors ([callouts.md](callouts.md)).

## Driving it

Preconditions:

- Use the baseline instance with its app, launched as [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) says, then `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"sidebar"}}'`. On Linux, run every command that reaches the app inside one `xvfb-run -a -s '-noreset -screen 0 1600x1000x24' bash -c '…'`. The app opens on home, and the baseline starts in desktop flow.
- Make a proof folder with `P=$(mktemp -d /tmp/drive-attn-sidebar.XXXX) && echo "export P=$P" >> $DRIVE_ENV` and an empty agent folder with `A=$(mktemp -d /tmp/drive-attn-sidebar-agent.XXXX) && echo "export A=$A" >> $DRIVE_ENV`.
- Start two agents with `wsctl add-session --cwd $A --agent claude --label side-a --id side-a --place`, then the same with `side-b`. With no session the sidebar starts collapsed. The first agent expands it. Open `side-b` from its row and press `mod+alt+2` to send it to desktop 2. `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` then lists two desktops: `<first desktop>` holding `side-a` and `<second desktop>` holding `side-b`.

- **Open an agent and a desktop from their rows.** `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.session.side-a"}}'` answers with the row "Open side-a". Run `bridge press` on it: `bridge state '{"of":"window"}'` shows `view: "Desktop"` and `on_screen: "side-a"`. Run `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.desktop.<second desktop>"}}'`: that desktop is current in `bridge state '{"of":"profiles"}'`. Run `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.home"}}'`: `view` is `Home`.
- **Collapse with the key and the title bar.** Press `bridge key '{"chord":"mod+b"}'`: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"rail"}}'` answers, with a `rail.desktop.<number>` chip for every desktop, and `sidebar_hidden` is true. Run `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"titlebar.sidebar"}}'`: the sidebar is back. Press it again: the rail again, and `mod+b` brings the sidebar back.
- **Collapse from the palette.** Press `mod+shift+k`, type `Hide the sidebar`, wait for `palette.row.command:toggle-sidebar` and press `Enter`: `rail` answers. Run the same command, now "Show the sidebar": `sidebar` is back.
- **The title bar's buttons.** Press `titlebar.commands`: `palette.dialog` answers. Press `Escape`. Press `titlebar.notifications`: `notifications.panel` answers. Press `notifications.close`: it goes. `titlebar.new`, the garden, notebook, ledger, automations and asks buttons and the profile pill open features of their own. To drive them, see [agent-launch.md](agent-launch.md), [garden.md](garden.md), [notebook.md](notebook.md), [ledger.md](ledger.md), [automations.md](automations.md), [asks.md](asks.md) and [profiles.md](profiles.md).
- **The queue sidebar and its agent list.** Run `./attn settings set queue_mode_enabled true`: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.queue"}}'` answers, with `queue-turn.side-a` and `queue-turn.side-b`, which are owed a turn since they booted. Settle `side-b` with `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"queue-turn.side-b.settle"}}'` ([queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md)). Press `mod+shift+a`: `queue-settled.side-b` shows. Run `bridge type '{"text":"zzz"}'`: the filter took the keyboard when the list opened, so `queue-settled.side-b` goes and the list reads "Nobody else.". Press `Escape`: the filter clears. Press `Escape` again: the list closes.
- **The queue bar.** Press `mod+b`: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"queue-bar.pill"}}'` answers, named "1 waiting · side-a". Run `bridge hover '{"target":{"key":"queue-bar.pill"}}'`: `queue-bar.peek` answers and its rows in `bridge tree` name `side-a` and `side-b`. Press the pill: the agent palette opens. Press `Escape`. Press `mod+shift+a`: the agent palette opens again, since the queue sidebar is not showing. Press `Escape`. Press `mod+b` again: `sidebar.queue` is back.
- **A row's actions.** Run `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"queue-turn.side-a.actions"}}'`: `bridge wait '{"target":{"role":"menu","name":"Actions for side-a"}}'` answers, with `session.menu.priority` ("Mark priority"), `session.menu.rename`, `session.menu.reload` and `session.menu.close`. Press `Escape`: the menu closes. Run `./attn settings set queue_mode_enabled false` and press `sidebar.session.side-a.actions`: the same items. Press `session.menu.priority`: `wsctl list` shows `"priority": true` on `side-a`, and the menu's first item then reads "Unmark priority". Press it to clear the flag. Press `session.menu.rename`, `bridge type '{"text":"renamed"}'` (the old name starts selected) and `bridge key '{"chord":"Enter"}'`: `./attn agent list` shows `renamed`. Rename it back the same way, pressing `session.rename.save` in place of Enter. Pressing `session.rename.cancel` closes the field and sends nothing.
- **Resize by the edge.** Run `bridge drag '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.resize"},"by":[60,0]}'`: `./attn settings get sidebar_width` prints 300, and `bridge find '{"target":{"key":"sidebar"}}'` is 300 wide. Drag `"by":[-400,0]`: the setting prints 200, the narrowest. Run `./attn settings set sidebar_width ""` for the default 240.
- **Width steps.** Press `mod+alt+]`: `./attn settings get sidebar_width` prints 280 and `bridge find '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.width-label"}}'` reads "280px" until it leaves on its own. Press `mod+alt+[` twice: 200, labelled "200px is the narrowest". Press it again: `bridge state '{"of":"window"}'` shows the notice "The sidebar can't go below 200px." and the setting stays. The palette's "Widen the sidebar" and "Narrow the sidebar" do the same, and "Reset sidebar width" empties the setting. Press `mod+b` and then `mod+alt+]`: the notice says the sidebar is hidden and the setting stays. Press `mod+b` and clear the setting.
- **Width setting.** Open Settings with `mod+,` and press `settings.nav.general`. Press `settings.setting.sidebar_width`, press `mod+a`, type `360` and press `Enter`: `./attn settings get sidebar_width` prints 360 and the sidebar moves. Type `900` and press `Enter`: the field shows the daemon's reason and the setting stays 360. Run `./attn settings set sidebar_width 300`: the field shows 300. Press `settings.sidebar-width.default`: `./attn settings get sidebar_width` prints nothing, the sidebar is 240 wide and the button goes. Run `./attn settings set sidebar_width 900`: it exits 1 with `sidebar_width must be a whole number from 200 to 600, got "900"`.
- **Reorder desktops.** Read the `rect` of `sidebar.desktop.<first desktop>` and of `sidebar.session.side-b`, the second desktop's last row, with `bridge find`. Run `bridge drag '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.desktop.<first desktop>"},"by":[0,<dy>]}'`, where `<dy>` is `side-b`'s bottom edge minus the first desktop row's middle: `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` lists the second desktop first. To put them back, read the rects again and drag the first desktop's row up by the second desktop row's top edge minus its own middle, a negative `<dy>`.
- **Proof.** In `$P`, keep `bridge state '{"of":"window"}'` after each step and `./attn settings get sidebar_width` after each width change, and capture `bridge shot '{"path":"<P>/desktop-sidebar.png"}'`, then `rail.png`, `queue-sidebar.png` and `queue-bar.png`, with `<P>` written out. Name each file for its way in, such as `sidebar-collapse-key-state.json`.

## Contexts

- **Durability across restarts**: the sidebar's width is a daemon setting and comes back with it. The shape follows the daemon's flow setting and comes back with it. Collapsed or expanded is not kept: a relaunch starts expanded when there are sessions and collapsed when there are none, with the agent list closed. Harness logos are a daemon setting.
- **Linux**: the title bar sits under the window's own title bar, with no space for traffic lights. `session.toggleSidebar` is Ctrl+Alt+B, `sidebar.agentList` is Ctrl+Alt+A, and `sidebar.wider` and `sidebar.narrower` are Ctrl+Alt+] and Ctrl+Alt+[ (the `LINUX` table in `native/crates/attn-app/src/keymap.rs`).
- **Outposts**: an agent whose outpost is offline keeps its row, marked offline, and the waiting count leaves it out.
- **Profiles**: the sidebar shows the selected profile's desktops, agents and crew only. The profile pill switches profiles ([profiles.md](profiles.md)). Flow is one setting for every profile today, so switching profiles keeps the shape.
- **Performance and idle cost**: turn ages refresh from the clock every 30 seconds, and a row's settling bar animates only while a countdown runs. Nothing measures the sidebar's idle cost. When a change touches the sidebar's rendering, check for repaints with `docs/internal/perf-testing.md`.
- **Harnesses**: each row carries its harness's mark. With logos off, the queue sidebar still names the harness in the row's hover text.
- **Keyboard and native input**: ⌘B, ⌘⇧A, ⌥⌘] and ⌥⌘[ reach the sidebar from a terminal or the window. On macOS a focused field keeps ⌘B for itself, while the others still fire. In the queue sidebar, ↑ and ↓ walk rows, and Escape clears the filter, then closes the list.
- **Flow**: queue flow shows the queue sidebar and the queue bar, and desktop flow shows the desktop sidebar and the rail. ⌘⇧A opens the agent list only where the queue sidebar shows. Both open sidebars share one width.
- **Accessibility**: the sidebar's list keeps focus while ↑ and ↓ move its reached row, and names that row as its active descendant, so VoiceOver reads each agent as the arrows reach it.

## Gotchas

- With the sidebar collapsed or a tile filling the window, ⌥⌘] and ⌥⌘[ change nothing and raise a notice. "Reset sidebar width" still clears the setting.
- On a fresh instance with no session, the sidebar is collapsed: the rail, or the queue bar in queue flow. It expands by itself when the first session appears, and collapses again when the last one goes. Launch an agent before judging the expanded shapes.
- The title bar has no Home button. Go home with ⌘⇧H, `rail.home` or `sidebar.home`.
- The agent list's filter takes the keyboard when the list opens, so ⌘↑, ⌘↓ and ⌘B do nothing until Escape or a press moves focus out of it.
- ⌘⇧A opens the agent palette in desktop flow, on the queue bar and while a tile fills the window. The palette is the designed result there, not a failure of the agent list.
- The drag that reorders desktops needs its `<dy>` from fresh rects. A stored rect no longer matches once a row moves.
- Working but wrong: the bell's label is "Show Notifications" while the palette says "Show notifications".
