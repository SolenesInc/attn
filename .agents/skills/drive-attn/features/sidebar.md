# Sidebar

The sidebar is the window's list of what the user works with. Its shape depends
on the profile's flow. In desktop flow, the sidebar is the desktop sidebar. It
shows each desktop with its agents and tiles, and collapses to a thin column of
desktop chips. In queue flow, the sidebar is the queue sidebar. It shows Home,
the crew, the agents that are owed a turn, the rest of the agents, automation
runs and desktop chips, and collapses to the queue bar across the top. The
title bar holds the profile, Commands, the flow switch and the buttons for the
garden, the notebook, the ledger, automations, notifications and asks, and the
queue bar's contents too. The sidebar has no header.

## Sub-features

- `desktop-sidebar`: desktop flow, with each desktop and its agents and tiles in layout order.
- `collapsed-rail`: the desktop sidebar collapsed to a column with Home and desktop chips.
- `queue-sidebar`: queue flow, with Home, crew, the waiting card with the oldest turns, automation runs and desktop chips.
- `queue-bar`: the queue sidebar collapsed into a strip: the waiting pill and its peek, asks, runs and desktop chips. The strip is part of the title bar, without its own profile and tools or the asks chip, and the shelf sits inside it ([desktops.md](desktops.md)).
- `agent-list`: every agent of the profile under the waiting card, with a filter.
- `collapse`: hide the sidebar down to the rail or the bar, and bring it back.
- `title-bar`: one 32px toolbar in the window's title bar. Left, beside the traffic lights: New, Commands, the flow switch, the sidebar toggle, notifications. Middle: the shelf, or in queue flow with the sidebar collapsed the queue bar's waiting pill, runs and desktop chips around it. Right: garden, notebook, ledger, automations, asks, the instance marker and the profile pill. The rail keeps Home and desktop chips.
- `resize`: drag the open sidebar's right edge. The tile area keeps its width during the drag and fits once on release. A double-click clears the width.
- `width-steps`: keys and palette commands move the open sidebar by 40px and name a limit they can't pass. The limits are 200px, 600px and the window less 480px for tiles.
- `width-setting`: the width in Settings → Appearance and in `sidebar_width`, with a way back to the default of 240px.
- `session-rows`: one row per agent with its state, harness mark and actions menu. A clipped name shows in full on hover. Clicking the row of an off-screen tile brings the tile beside the active tile, and hovering the row opens its peek panel ([desktops.md](desktops.md)).

## How to get to it

### People

- Keys (`app/src/shortcuts/registry.ts`): ⌘B (`session.toggleSidebar`) collapses and expands the sidebar. ⌘⇧A (`sidebar.agentList`) opens and closes the agent list. Where the queue sidebar is not showing, ⌘⇧A opens the agent palette instead (`app/src/application/useAppController.tsx`). ⌥⌘] (`sidebar.wider`) and ⌥⌘[ (`sidebar.narrower`) step the width. They follow the physical keys on every layout ([shortcuts.md](shortcuts.md)).
- Palette (`app/src/application/useAppCommands.tsx`): `toggle-sidebar` ("Hide the sidebar" or "Show the sidebar"), and `sidebar-wider` ("Widen the sidebar"), `sidebar-narrower` ("Narrow the sidebar") and `sidebar-reset-width` ("Reset sidebar width").
- Title bar (`app/src/components/TitleBar.tsx`, `app/src/components/TitleBarControls.tsx`): `[aria-label="Collapse sidebar"]`, `[aria-label="New agent"]`, `button[aria-label="Commands"]`, the flow button, and the profile pill. Its panel buttons are named by what they do, such as "Show the garden" and "Show Notifications" (`app/src/application/useAppSidebarActions.tsx`). What each button opens is described in that button's feature file.
- Title bar (`app/src/components/TitleBar.tsx`): `[data-testid="title-bar"]`, with `[data-testid="title-bar-left"]` and `[data-testid="title-bar-right"]` holding the same `aria-label`s as the header, and `[aria-label="Show sidebar"]` when collapsed. When the desktop chips don't fit in the room measured, the queue bar in the band shows only the current desktop's chip and `[data-testid="queue-bar-desktop-extras"]`, whose `+N` opens the overview. A press on an empty part drags the window, and a double-click zooms it (`data-tauri-drag-region="deep"`). Panels hanging from the band opt out with `data-tauri-drag-region="false"`. While a surface holds the window, such as the Notebook, the Ledger, the full Garden or a dialog, the band stays visible and draggable but its controls take no clicks, because the surface's focus trap would swallow them. `set_title_bar_overlay` in `app/src-tauri/core/src/title_bar.rs` switches the native title bar live.
- Desktop sidebar and rail (`app/src/components/Sidebar.tsx`, `app/src/components/SidebarChrome.tsx`, `app/src/components/SidebarDesktops.tsx`, `app/src/components/SidebarRows.tsx`): rows `[data-testid="sidebar-session-<session>"]` with `[aria-label="Open <name>"]` and the actions button `[data-testid="session-actions-<session>"]`, which queue rows carry too. Desktop groups are `[data-testid="sidebar-desktop-<desktop>"]`, and the rail has `button[title="Expand sidebar"]`.
- Handle (`app/src/components/SidebarResizeHandle.tsx`, `app/src/application/useSidebarWidth.ts`): `.sidebar-resize-handle` shows only with the sidebar open and no tile maximized. Drag it to change the width, press Escape, leave the window or collapse the sidebar mid-drag to cancel, and double-click it to clear the width. `.sidebar-resize-label` shows the width during a drag and after a key step, and names a limit at one. The page of a browser tile in the first column starts just past the handle, so all of the handle takes a press.
- Settings (`app/src/components/SettingsModalSections.tsx`): Appearance has `[data-testid="settings-sidebar-width"]`. `[data-testid="settings-sidebar-width-reset"]` shows while the width differs from 240 and clears the setting. A refused value shows the daemon's message.
- Queue sidebar and bar (`app/src/components/QueueSidebar.tsx`, `app/src/components/QueueBar.tsx`, `app/src/components/QueueRows.tsx`): `[data-testid="queue-agents-toggle"]` and the filter `[aria-label="Filter agents"]`. ↑ and ↓ walk the rows, a letter typed with the list open filters it, and Escape clears the filter, then closes the list, then returns to the agent on screen. On the bar, `[aria-label="Show sidebar"]` expands it, and the waiting pill `[data-testid="queue-bar-pill"]` opens the agent palette. Hovering the pill shows a peek of the agents.
- Every row's actions menu: "Mark priority", "Rename session", "Reload session" and "Close session" (`app/src/components/SessionActionsPopover.tsx`). See [queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md), [ledger.md](ledger.md), [crew.md](crew.md) and [agent-launch.md](agent-launch.md) for what each does.
- Driven in other files: ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ step through what the sidebar lists, desktops in desktop flow ([desktops.md](desktops.md)) and the queue in queue flow ([queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md)). People switch, rename, close and drag between desktops here ([desktops.md](desktops.md)). The flow button changes the shape ([queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md)). The dock of shortcut chips sits at the foot of the desktop sidebar ([shortcuts.md](shortcuts.md)). See [harnesses.md](harnesses.md) for the harness logos on each row, and see [instances.md](instances.md) for the instance marker in the header.
- CLI: `attn settings set sidebar_width <200-600>` moves the width live, and an empty value clears it.
- Where people see what agents do: each row's state dot, harness mark, priority flag and turn age, the waiting count, and the waiting dot on desktop chips.

### Agents

None. Agents change what the sidebar shows without driving it: `attn session rename` sets the name a row shows ([ledger.md](ledger.md)), and `attn callout` can point at the `sidebar`, `home`, `commands`, `garden`, `desktops`, `queue` and `crew` anchors ([callouts.md](callouts.md)).

## Driving it

Preconditions:

- Use the baseline instance with its app. Check that `bridge --launch --wait-ready ping` answers, then run `bridge dismiss_whats_new` to dismiss What's new. The baseline starts in desktop flow.
- Make a proof folder with `P=$(mktemp -d /tmp/drive-attn-sidebar.XXXX) && echo "export P=$P" >> $DRIVE_ENV` and an empty agent folder with `A=$(mktemp -d /tmp/drive-attn-sidebar-agent.XXXX) && echo "export A=$A" >> $DRIVE_ENV`.
- Start two agents with `wsctl add-session --cwd $A --agent claude --label side-a --id side-a`, then the same with `side-b`. With no session the sidebar starts collapsed. The first agent expands it.

- **Open an agent from its row.** Run `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"[data-testid=\"sidebar-session-side-a\"]","timeoutMs":5000}'`, then click `[aria-label="Open side-a"]` natively, using the point from `bridge dom_native_point`. `bridge get_state` shows `activeLeaf.sessionId: "side-a"`.
- **Collapse with the key.** Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"b","modifiers":["command"]}'`. `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":".sidebar.collapsed","timeoutMs":5000}'` matches, and the rail still has `[aria-label="Home"]` and `button[aria-label="Commands"]`. Press ⌘B again: `[data-testid="sidebar-home"]` is back.
- **Collapse from the palette and the title bar.** Open the palette with ⌘⇧K, type `Hide the sidebar` and press Enter: `.sidebar.collapsed` matches. Click `button[title="Expand sidebar"]` natively: it expands. Click `[aria-label="Collapse sidebar"]` natively: it collapses again, and ⌘B brings it back.
- **The title bar's buttons.** Click `button[aria-label="Commands"]` natively: `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"[role=\"combobox\"][aria-label=\"Commands\"]","focused":true,"timeoutMs":5000}'` matches. Press Escape. Click `[aria-label="Show Notifications"]` natively: `bridge notifications_get_state` shows `present: true`. Click `[aria-label="Hide Notifications"]` natively: the popover stays open and `present` stays true, because the popover's backdrop covers the bell (see Gotchas). Open the palette, type `Hide notifications` and press Enter: `present` is false. `[aria-label="New agent"]`, the garden, notebook, ledger, automations and asks buttons and the profile pill open features of their own. To drive them, see [agent-launch.md](agent-launch.md), [garden.md](garden.md), [notebook.md](notebook.md), [ledger.md](ledger.md), [automations.md](automations.md), [asks.md](asks.md) and [profiles.md](profiles.md).
- **The queue sidebar.** Run `./attn settings set queue_mode_enabled true`. `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"[data-testid=\"sidebar-queue\"]","timeoutMs":5000}'` matches, and `bridge queue_get_state` shows `present: true` and `turns` naming `side-a` and `side-b`, which are owed a turn since they booted. Settle `side-b` for setup: select it with `bridge select_session '{"sessionId":"side-b"}'` and press ⌘⇧E ([queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md)). `turns` now names only `side-a`.
- **The agent list.** Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"a","modifiers":["command","shift"]}'`. `bridge queue_get_state` shows `agentList.expanded: true` and `settled` naming `side-b`, and `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"[data-testid=\"queue-agent-filter\"]","focused":true,"timeoutMs":5000}'` matches. Run `bridge native_text '{"text":"zzz"}'`: `settled` is empty and the list reads `Nobody else.`. Press Escape: the filter clears. Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"arrowdown"}'`: `bridge dom_focus_state` shows focus on the first row, `BUTTON` labelled `Open side-a`. Press `arrowdown` again for `Open side-b` and `arrowup` for `Open side-a`. Press Escape: `agentList.expanded` is false. Press Escape again: focus returns to the agent on screen, the `Terminal input` of `side-a`.
- **The queue bar.** Press ⌘B. `bridge queue_get_state` shows `present: false` and `bar.present: true`, with `bar.waiting: 1` and `bar.crumbs` naming `side-a`. Move over `[data-testid="queue-bar-pill"]` with `bridge native_mouse` `move`: `bridge queue_get_state` shows `bar.waitingPeek` naming `agent:side-a` and `agent:side-b`. Click the pill natively: `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"[role=\"combobox\"][aria-label=\"Agents\"]","focused":true,"timeoutMs":5000}'` matches. Press Escape. Press ⌘⇧A: the agent palette opens again, since the queue sidebar is not showing. Press Escape. Click `[aria-label="Show sidebar"]` natively: `present` is true again.
- **A row's actions.** Move over `.queue-row[data-session-id="side-a"]` with `bridge native_mouse`, then click `[data-testid="session-actions-side-a"]` natively. `bridge dom_text '{"selector":"[role=\"menu\"][aria-label=\"Actions for side-a\"]"}'` lists `Mark priority`, `Rename session`, `Reload session` and `Close session`. Press Escape: the menu closes. Run `./attn settings set queue_mode_enabled false`, move over `[data-testid="sidebar-session-side-a"]` and click `[data-testid="session-actions-side-a"]` natively: the desktop row's menu lists the same four actions. Click its first item, `Mark priority`, natively: `bridge dom_text '{"selector":"[data-testid=\"sidebar-session-side-a\"]"}'` reads `⚑side-a`. Open the menu again: its first item reads `Unmark priority`. Click it and the flag goes.
- **Resize.** Run `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":".sidebar-resize-handle","timeoutMs":5000}'`. Press on the handle with `bridge native_mouse` `press`, move with `drag_to` and `bridge dom_text '{"selector":".sidebar-resize-label"}'` reads the width. Release: `./attn settings get sidebar_width` reads it, and `bridge dom_bounds '{"selector":".sidebar"}'` shows it. Double-click: the setting reads empty and the sidebar is 240px.
- **Resize beside a browser tile.** Run `./attn browser open <page url> --session side-a`. Click the tile's header natively and press ⇧⌥⌘← (`shelf.carryLeft`) until `bridge get_desktop '{"sessionId":"side-a"}'` lists the tile first in `desktop.layoutTree`. `bridge dom_bounds` shows `.browser-tile-host` 5px past the tile's left edge. Resolve the handle's tile side with `bridge dom_native_point '{"selector":".sidebar-resize-handle","at":{"x":0.9,"y":0.5}}'`, then `native_mouse` `press` there and `drag_to` 80px right: `.sidebar-resize-label` reads `320px`. Press Escape: the sidebar returns to its width and the page shows under the pointer. `native_mouse` hit-tests every event, so `drag_to` back onto the handle before `release`, or the release lands in the page. A native click at `{"selector":".browser-tile-host","at":{"x":0,"y":0.5},"offset":{"x":1,"y":0}}` reaches the page.
- **Width steps.** With a terminal focused, press ⌥⌘] (`bridge native_key '{"key":"]","modifiers":["command","option"]}'`): `./attn settings get sidebar_width` prints 280, `bridge dom_bounds '{"selector":".sidebar"}'` shows 280, `.sidebar-resize-label` reads `280px` and leaves on its own, and `./attn agent peek` shows nothing typed. Press ⌥⌘[ (`"key":"["`) twice: 200, and the label reads `200px is the narrowest`. Press it again: the toast reads `The sidebar can't go below 200px.` and the setting stays. Run `./attn settings set sidebar_width 600` and press ⌥⌘]: the toast names 600px, or the window's limit in a window under 1080px. The palette's "Widen the sidebar" and "Narrow the sidebar" do the same, and "Reset sidebar width" empties the setting. Press ⌘B and then ⌥⌘]: the toast says the sidebar is hidden and the setting stays. Press ⌘B to bring it back.
- **Title bar.** `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"[data-testid=\"title-bar\"]","timeoutMs":5000}'` matches, and `bridge dom_bounds '{"selector":".desktop-pane"}'` shows tiles at y 32. Click each control natively with its point from `bridge dom_native_point`, as `app/scripts/real-app-harness/scenario-title-bar.mjs` does. Press ⌘B to see the rail, and with `queue_mode_enabled` on, the queue bar inside the band.
- **Width setting.** Press ⌘, and click `[data-testid="settings-nav-general"]` natively. Click `[data-testid="settings-sidebar-width"]` natively, press ⌘A, type `360` and press Enter: `./attn settings get sidebar_width` prints 360 and the sidebar moves. Type `900` and press Enter: `.settings-warning` shows `sidebar_width must be a whole number from 200 to 600, got "900"` and the setting stays 360. Type `360` again and press Enter. Run `./attn settings set sidebar_width 300`: the field shows 300 and the sidebar moves. Click `[data-testid="settings-sidebar-width-reset"]` natively: `./attn settings get sidebar_width` prints nothing, the sidebar is 240px and the button leaves. The field is empty until a relaunch, when the daemon's snapshot fills it with 240. Run `./attn settings set sidebar_width 900`: it exits 1 with the same message.
- **Proof.** In `$P`, keep `bridge get_state` after each step and `bridge queue_get_state` after each shape change, and capture each shape: `bridge capture_screenshot '{"path":"<P>/desktop-sidebar.png"}'`, then `rail.png`, `queue-sidebar.png` and `queue-bar.png`, with `<P>` written out. Name each file for its way in, such as `sidebar-collapse-key-state.json`.

### In the native app

The native app (`native/`) draws both sidebars, the rail, the queue bar, the agent list and its filter, each row's actions menu, the resize edge, the width keys and Settings → Appearance's width field, drags desktops into a new order, and shows an unread critical notification as a strip above Home ([home.md](home.md)). Of the title bar's feature buttons it draws the bell and asks. The others, and resizing beside a browser tile, drive only in the Tauri app. `bridge` reaches the native app's own automation actions (`docs/internal/native-app.md`, Automation).

Preconditions:

- Install the instance with `make install INSTANCE=<name> APP=native`. On Linux, run every command that reaches the app inside one `xvfb-run -a -s '-noreset -screen 0 1600x1000x24' bash -c '…'`.
- Launch as [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) says for a native instance, then `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"sidebar"}}'`. The native app opens on home.
- Make `P` and `A` and start `side-a` and `side-b` as above, each with `--place`. Open `side-b` from its row and press `mod+alt+2` to send it to desktop 2. `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` then lists two desktops: `<first desktop>` holding `side-a` and `<second desktop>` holding `side-b`.

Steps:

- **Open an agent and a desktop from their rows.** `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.session.side-a"}}'` answers with the row "Open side-a". Run `bridge press` on it: `bridge state '{"of":"window"}'` shows `view: "Desktop"` and `on_screen: "side-a"`. Run `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.desktop.<second desktop>"}}'`: that desktop is current in `bridge state '{"of":"profiles"}'`. Run `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.home"}}'`: `view` is `Home`.
- **Collapse with the key and the title bar.** Press `bridge key '{"chord":"mod+b"}'`: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"rail"}}'` answers and `sidebar_hidden` is true. Run `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"rail.expand"}}'`: the sidebar is back. Run `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"titlebar.sidebar"}}'`: the rail again, and `mod+b` brings the sidebar back.
- **The queue sidebar and its agent list.** Run `./attn settings set queue_mode_enabled true`: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.queue"}}'` answers, with `queue-turn.side-a` and `queue-turn.side-b`. Settle `side-b` with `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"queue-turn.side-b.settle"}}'`. Press `mod+shift+a`: `queue-settled.side-b` shows. Run `bridge type '{"text":"zzz"}'`: the filter took the keyboard when the list opened, so `queue-settled.side-b` goes and the list reads "Nobody else.". Press `Escape`: the filter clears. Press `Escape` again: the list closes.
- **The queue bar.** Press `mod+b`: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"queue-bar.pill"}}'` answers, named "1 waiting · side-a". Press `mod+b` again: `sidebar.queue` is back.
- **A row's actions.** Run `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"queue-turn.side-a.actions"}}'`: `bridge wait '{"target":{"role":"menu","name":"Actions for side-a"}}'` answers, with `session.menu.priority` ("Mark priority"), `session.menu.rename`, `session.menu.reload` and `session.menu.close`. Press `Escape`: the menu closes. Run `./attn settings set queue_mode_enabled false` and press `sidebar.session.side-a.actions`: the same items. Press `session.menu.priority`: `wsctl list` shows `"priority": true` on `side-a`, and the menu's first item then reads "Unmark priority"; press it to clear the flag. Press `session.menu.rename`, `bridge type '{"text":"renamed"}'` (the old name starts selected) and `bridge key '{"chord":"Enter"}'`: `./attn agent list` shows `renamed`. Rename it back the same way.
- **Resize by the edge.** Run `bridge drag '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.resize"},"by":[60,0]}'`: `./attn settings get sidebar_width` prints 300, and `bridge find '{"target":{"key":"sidebar"}}'` is 300 wide. Drag `"by":[-400,0]`: the setting prints 200, the narrowest. Run `./attn settings set sidebar_width ""` for the default 240.
- **Width steps.** Press `mod+alt+]`: `./attn settings get sidebar_width` prints 280 and `bridge find '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.width-label"}}'` reads "280px" until it leaves on its own. Press `mod+alt+[` twice: 200, labelled "200px is the narrowest". Press it again: `bridge state '{"of":"window"}'` shows the notice "The sidebar can't go below 200px." and the setting stays. Press `mod+b` and then `mod+alt+]`: the notice says the sidebar is hidden and the setting stays. Press `mod+b` and clear the setting.
- **Reorder desktops.** Read the `rect` of `sidebar.desktop.<first desktop>` and of `sidebar.session.side-b`, the second desktop's last row, with `bridge find`. Run `bridge drag '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.desktop.<first desktop>"},"by":[0,<dy>]}'`, where `<dy>` is `side-b`'s bottom edge minus the first desktop row's middle: `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` lists the second desktop first, and the Chief's desktop stays last. To put them back, read the rects again and drag the first desktop's row up by the second desktop row's top edge minus its own middle, a negative `<dy>`.
- **Proof.** In `$P`, keep `bridge state '{"of":"window"}'` after each step and `./attn settings get sidebar_width` after each width change, and capture `bridge shot '{"path":"<P>/native-desktop-sidebar.png"}'`, then `native-rail.png`, `native-queue-sidebar.png` and `native-queue-bar.png`, with `<P>` written out.

## Contexts

- **Durability across restarts**: the sidebar's width is a daemon setting and comes back with it. The shape follows the daemon's flow setting and comes back with it. Collapsed or expanded is not kept: a relaunch starts expanded when there are sessions and collapsed when there are none, with the agent list closed. Harness logos are a daemon setting.
- **Linux**: the title bar sits under the native title bar, with no space for traffic lights. `session.toggleSidebar` is Ctrl+Alt+B, `sidebar.agentList` is Ctrl+Alt+A, and `sidebar.wider` and `sidebar.narrower` are Ctrl+Alt+] and Ctrl+Alt+[ (`LINUX_SHORTCUTS`).
- **Outposts**: an agent whose outpost is offline keeps its row, marked offline, and the waiting count leaves it out.
- **Profiles**: the sidebar shows the selected profile's desktops, agents and crew only. The profile pill switches profiles ([profiles.md](profiles.md)). Flow is one setting for every profile today, so switching profiles keeps the shape.
- **Performance and idle cost**: turn ages refresh from the clock every 30 seconds, and a row's settling bar animates only while a countdown runs. Nothing measures the sidebar's idle cost. When a change touches the sidebar's rendering, check for repaints with `docs/internal/perf-testing.md`.
- **Harnesses**: each row carries its harness's mark. With logos off, the queue sidebar still names the harness in the row's hover text.
- **Keyboard and native input**: ⌘B, ⌘⇧A, ⌥⌘] and ⌥⌘[ reach the sidebar from a terminal or the window. In an input, a textarea or an editor outside a terminal, ⌘B stays with that field, while the others still fire. In the queue sidebar, ↑ and ↓ walk rows, and Escape clears the filter, then closes the list, then returns to the agent on screen.
- **Flow**: queue flow shows the queue sidebar and the queue bar, and desktop flow shows the desktop sidebar and the rail. ⌘⇧A opens the agent list only where the queue sidebar shows. Both open sidebars share one width.

## Gotchas

- With the sidebar collapsed or a tile filling the window, ⌥⌘] and ⌥⌘[ change nothing and raise a toast. "Reset sidebar width" still clears the setting.
- The open notifications popover's full-window backdrop (`.notifications-panel-backdrop`) covers `[aria-label="Hide Notifications"]` (seed `s-7dw2fy`). A native click on the bell leaves `bridge notifications_get_state` at `present: true`, so the header's hide step fails by design. The palette's `Hide notifications` closes the popover before another independent check.
- On a fresh instance with no session, the sidebar is collapsed: the rail, or the queue bar in queue flow. It expands by itself when the first session appears, and collapses again when the last one goes. Launch an agent before judging the expanded shapes.
- The title bar has the header buttons, but not Home. Go home with ⌘⇧H and open drawers from the palette or their keys while it shows.
- `[aria-label="Commands"]` also names the palette's input and dialog. Click `button[aria-label="Commands"]`.
- The agent list's filter takes focus when the list opens, so ⌘↑, ⌘↓ and ⌘B do nothing until Escape or a click moves focus out of it.
- ⌘⇧A opens the agent palette in desktop flow, on the queue bar and while a tile fills the window. The palette is the designed result there, not a failure of the agent list.
- The waiting card shows only the turns that fit. The rest move into the agent list as "Also waiting". A short window hides turns from the card, not from the queue.
- A row's buttons (settle, snooze, unsnooze, actions) take clicks only while the pointer is on the row. After a native click on one, keyboard focus stays on that row, and ⌘⇧E, ⌘⇧S and ⌘⇧I act on that row, not on the agent on screen.
- `bridge queue_get_state` reads `settled` and `snoozed` rows only while the agent list is open.
