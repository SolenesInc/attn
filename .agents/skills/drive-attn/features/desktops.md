# Desktops and tiles

Desktops are arrangements of tiles, like macOS Spaces: switch, split, sort,
and move tiles between desktops. Each profile has its own desktops, and every
window shows the profile's current desktop. A tile is one rectangle on a
desktop: a terminal tile, or a browser, markdown, seed or editor tile. People
arrange desktops from the keyboard, the sidebar and the overview. Agents move
their own terminals and shells, and crew members and automations start their
sessions on a launch desktop.

## Sub-features

- `desktop-switch`: go to a desktop with ⌘1–⌘9, the sidebar, the overview or the palette. The current desktop's digit goes back to the previous desktop.
- `desktop-overview`: every desktop as a card, with keys to switch, send the active tile and create.
- `desktop-new`: create a desktop, which takes the lowest free number. ⌘<n> or a move to a free digit creates that numbered desktop.
- `desktop-rename`: name a desktop from the sidebar.
- `desktop-close`: close a desktop and everything on it, after a confirmation. ⌘W closes an empty desktop.
- `desktop-sort`: numbered desktops first, then names A–Z, with Undo. Drag a desktop in the sidebar to reorder it.
- `empty-desktop-sweep`: the daemon removes an unnamed desktop 30 seconds after the desktop is both empty and not current.
- `tile-split`: open a shell beside or below the active terminal tile.
- `tile-focus`: move the active tile with arrows. The focus style marks the active tile (dim, rail or spotlight).
- `tile-zoom`: zoom the active tile, or focus it alone on the desktop.
- `tile-move`: send the active tile to another desktop and follow or stay. Drag a tile. Agents move terminals. Move an agent with its delegates.
- `tile-history`: walk back and forward through the tiles this window showed.
- `tile-close`: close the active tile.
- `layout-settings`: the narrowest tile width in columns and the sidebar width, in Settings → Appearance ([sidebar.md](sidebar.md)).
- `shelf-row`: the screen shows a run of the row's columns and the shelf in the title bar lists the rest ([sidebar.md](sidebar.md)). ⌥⌘←→ slide along the row and leave for the next desktop past its end. ⌥⌘− and ⌥⌘= step the narrowest width until one fewer or one more tile fits, and ⌥⌘= clears the dragged widths on screen when they are what blocks another tile.
- `shelf-bring`: a chip, a cap's menu and peek's Open bring an off-screen tile on the current desktop next to the active tile, which keeps its screen position: the tile lands on its right, or on its left when it is rightmost on screen. With one tile on screen, or when the tile's dragged width cannot fit beside the active one, they jump instead. ⌘J, the palette, a sidebar click and queue steps always jump: the row slides to show the tile and its order stays. ⌘[ and ⌘] slide to a tile without reordering.
- `shelf-carry`: ⇧⌥⌘←→ carry the active column along the row.
- `shelf-send`: ⇧⌥⌘↑ sends the active tile to the shelf: a stacked tile to the row's end, a single tile past the screen's far side, or a held column when it would have fit.
- `shelf-open`: ⌘D, ⌘N and ⌘T open beside the active column. ⌘⇧D and ⌘⇧N open below it until the 320px height limit, then beside with a message.
- `shelf-close`: ⌘W, an agent's exit and `attn terminal close` go to the most recently used tile still on the desktop, and the row slides to show it. Position decides only when the desktop has no focus history, as right after an upgrade.
- `shelf-agent-terminals`: a terminal an agent starts goes to the row's end and takes no focus. `attn terminal open --down` stacks it below the agent.
- `shelf-documents`: a document an agent opens slides into view if the active tile stays on screen. Otherwise its chip counts as waiting and ⌘J takes it first. A document that arrives while its desktop is not mounted gets the same treatment when the user returns. So does a document, seed or browser tile already off screen that an agent opens again. Closing the document restores the screen.
- `shelf-drags`: a chip on a tile's center swaps the two columns. A chip on a tile's edge places or stacks its column, and between chips it reorders the row without taking focus. A tile dropped beside a chip moves to that side of the chip's column and comes on screen with it, active, clearing a dragged width of either that keeps them apart; when two narrowest tiles do not fit, it shows alone with a message; on a cap it goes beside the row's first or last column, and a stacked tile leaves its stack (the `native-shelf-drop-beside` scenario covers every drop place). A chip on a sidebar desktop moves its whole column there and follows it. Dragging a divider between columns sets wanted widths, past the narrowest width it moves the far column to the shelf, and dragging back restores it. Double-clicking a divider clears the widths beside it, and "Clear dragged widths" clears them across the desktop. Esc cancels any of these drags.
- `shelf-peek`: hovering a chip shows a panel of that terminal's latest text and a reply box, with no terminal mounted and no layout change. It opens only when the pointer moved onto the chip, never for one that layout brought under a still pointer. A stacked column's chip has one mark per tile: moving onto a mark peeks that tile and underlines the mark, and the rest of the chip peeks the column's last-used tile without replacing a mark's peek. ↵ sends a typed reply and closes the panel, or opens the tile when the box is empty. When the agent has stopped or its outpost is not reachable, ↵ sends nothing: the box keeps the reply and a notice above it gives the reason. When the terminal does not confirm a sent reply, a toast says so and the reply is in the box the next time the panel opens. Open carries a reply that was never submitted to the tile's notice, which offers Copy and Discard. When Return failed, Open on a live terminal drops the copy, because the text is in the terminal's input; when the agent has stopped or its outpost is not reachable, the tile keeps it. An agent that stops makes a reply whose Return failed an unsent one: the tile shows it however the user reaches it, and after a resume ↵ in the panel sends the whole reply, never Return alone, and nothing goes out on its own. Esc closes the panel and discards a reply that was never submitted. Any on-screen browser tile hides while the panel shows. A document or browser tile's panel has only its title and kind.
- `shelf-motion`: a layout change moves tiles on one spring. A slide moves every tile together, a new tile grows and fades in, and a closing tile shrinks and fades out, holding its terminal's last frame. Browser tiles snap, and a divider drag, focus mode and `prefers-reduced-motion` never animate.
- `launch-desktop`: the desktop where a crew member's or an automation's sessions start.

## How to get to it

### People

- Keys (`native/crates/attn-app/src/keymap.rs`, ids in `action.rs`): ⌘1–⌘9 (`desktop.select1`–`desktop.select9`), ⌥⌘1–⌥⌘9 move the active tile and follow (`desktop.send1`–`desktop.send9`), ⇧⌥⌘1–⇧⌥⌘9 move it and stay (`desktop.sendStay1`–`desktop.sendStay9`), ⌘G (`desktop.overview`), ⌘D and ⌘⇧D split (`terminal.splitVertical`, `terminal.splitHorizontal`), ⌘⇧Z zoom (`terminal.toggleZoom`), ⌘⇧↩ focus (`terminal.toggleMaximize`), ⌥⌘←→↑↓ (`terminal.focusLeft|Right|Up|Down`), ⌥⌘− and ⌥⌘= (`shelf.fewerTiles`, `shelf.moreTiles`), ⇧⌥⌘←→ (`shelf.carryLeft`, `shelf.carryRight`), ⇧⌥⌘↑ (`shelf.sendToShelf`), ⇧⌘O pops out the active tile or brings it back (`tile.popOut`), the sidebar width's ⌥⌘] and ⌥⌘[ ([sidebar.md](sidebar.md)), ⌘W (`terminal.close`, `session.close`), ⌘[ and ⌘] (`session.historyBack`, `session.historyForward`), and in desktop flow ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ (`session.prev`, `session.next`).
- Native menu (`native/crates/attn-app/src/lib.rs`): File → "Close Pane" runs `session.close`. Close Pane closes the focused markdown, seed, editor or browser tile. Otherwise Close Pane closes the terminal on screen and ends the terminal's shell or agent. On an empty desktop, Close Pane closes the desktop. File → "Pop Out or Bring Back Tile" runs `tile.popOut`. Neither item carries a key: ⌘W and ⇧⌘O reach the window's keymap.
- Palette (`native/crates/attn-app/src/palette/commands.rs`): `desktop-overview`, `new-desktop`, `close-desktop`, `sort-desktops`, `desktop-<id>` ("Go to <desktop>"), `move-with-delegates-<id>` ("Move with delegates to <desktop>", offered while the agent on screen has a delegate on this desktop), `tile-pop-out` ("Pop out tile"), `tile-bring-back` ("Bring back tile"), and `tile-focus-dim`, `tile-focus-rail`, `tile-focus-spotlight`, and `shelf-fewer-tiles`, `shelf-more-tiles`, `shelf-carry-left`, `shelf-carry-right`, `shelf-send-to-shelf`, `shelf-clear-dragged-widths`. A row's key is `palette.row.command:<id>`.
- Sidebar (`native/crates/attn-app/src/sidebar/mod.rs`): the "Desktop overview" row (`sidebar.overview`), each desktop's row ("Open <desktop>", `sidebar.desktop.<id>`), its "Rename <desktop>" and "Close <desktop>" buttons (`sidebar.desktop.<id>.rename`, `sidebar.desktop.<id>.close`), dragging a desktop's row to reorder it (`sidebar/reorder.rs`), and dragging an agent's row onto another desktop (`native/crates/attn-app/src/desktop/drag.rs`). Dropping the row with ⌥ held brings the agent's delegates along. Each tile's row under its desktop has a "Close <title>" button, and a browser tile's row has a "Reload <title>" button. Hover a row before you press its buttons. Closing a desktop that holds tiles asks first (`native/crates/attn-app/src/desktop/close.rs`).
- Overview (`native/crates/attn-app/src/desktop/overview.rs`): arrows move, ↵ switches, ⇧↵ or "Send focused pane here ⇧↵" sends the active tile, digits switch, Esc closes, "+ New desktop" (`overview.new`) creates.
- Tile headers (`native/crates/attn-app/src/desktop/view.rs`): "Close tile" on a document or browser tile, "Focus agent <name>" (`tile.<leaf>.focus`), "Return to row" (`tile.<leaf>.return`), "Pop out tile" (`tile.<leaf>.popout`), "Rename session <title>", and dragging a header (`tile.<leaf>.header`) to another place or desktop (`desktop/drag.rs`). As the tile narrows, the header shortens its pieces in a fixed order (`desktop/fit.rs`). The dot, the title and the signals stay.
- Shelf (`native/crates/attn-app/src/desktop/shelf.rs`, in the title bar): each chip (`shelf.chip.<leaf>`), the caps and their menus (`shelf.cap.<side>`, `shelf.menu.<side>`), the live dots, the width control (`shelf.width`, with `shelf.width.fewer` and `shelf.width.more`), hovering a chip for the peek panel (`shelf.peek`, `desktop/peek.rs`), and dragging a chip or dropping a tile on the shelf (`desktop/drag.rs`). Column dividers (`divider.<split>`, `desktop/divider.rs`) are on the tile area.
- Launch desktop field in the crew launch tab and the automation form (`native/crates/attn-app/src/launch_desktop.rs`): a select named "Desktop", keyed `crew.launch.<member>.desktop` and `automations.form.<id>.desktop`.
- Where people see what agents do: the sidebar lists a terminal that an agent opens or moves under the desktop the terminal sits on. An empty desktop shows the launcher (`launcher`, `native/crates/attn-app/src/picker/view.rs`).
- Their own shell: the `--launch-desktop` verbs below. `attn terminal` refuses without a session.

### Agents

- `attn terminal move [<terminal>] <desktop>` (`cli/attn/src/commands/terminal/mod.rs`): moves the caller's terminal, or a shell, or an agent below the caller in the caller's delegation chain. `<desktop>` is a digit, a name or an id. The command creates a missing numbered desktop. `attn terminal open --desktop <ref>` is in [terminals.md](terminals.md).
- `attn crew set <member> --launch-desktop` (`internal/catalog/content/crew.yaml`) and `attn automation set <id> --launch-desktop`, `attn automation apply --file <yaml> --launch-desktop` (`internal/catalog/content/automation.yaml`).
- `attn delegate --desktop` places a delegate ([delegation.md](delegation.md)), and `attn callout show desktop:<which>` points at a desktop ([callouts.md](callouts.md)).
- Prompts: placement, reach and moves in `attn skill --reference terminal` (`internal/prompts/content/skills/attn/references/terminal.md`).

### Triggers

- The daemon removes a desktop once it has been empty and not the current desktop for 30 seconds, if the desktop is also unnamed and not any item's launch desktop (`internal/daemon/empty_desktops.go`).
- A crew start or an automation run starts the session on the member's or the automation's launch desktop (`internal/daemon/launch_desktops.go`). Crew and automations own the start and the run.

## Driving it

Keys differ by platform. On macOS a desktop is `mod+<n>`, zoom `mod+shift+z`, focus `mod+shift+enter`, history `mod+[` and `mod+]`, and the overview `mod+g`. On Linux they are `mod+shift+<n>`, `mod+alt+z`, `mod+alt+enter`, `mod+shift+[` and `mod+shift+]`, and `mod+shift+g`. Send each one with `bridge key '{"chord":"<chord>"}'`.

Preconditions:

- Use the baseline instance with its app, launched as [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) says. Wait with `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"sidebar"}}'`. On Linux, run every command that reaches the app inside one `xvfb-run -a -s '-noreset -screen 0 1600x1000x24' bash -c '…'` so the display outlives each step.
- Two tiles at the default narrowest width (`narrowest_tile_cols` 66) need about 1200 points of tile area, and the window opens at 1200 by 800, which fits one. Widen it on either platform with `bridge set_window_bounds '{"logicalBounds":{"x":0,"y":0,"width":1600,"height":1000}}'`: it answers once the tiles lay out at the new size, or fails naming the size the window got.
- Make a proof folder with `P=$(mktemp -d /tmp/drive-attn-desktops.XXXX) && echo "export P=$P" >> $DRIVE_ENV`, and save `check.yaml` in it: `api_version: attn.dev/automations/v1alpha1`, `name: Local check`, `trigger: {type: manual}`, `prompt: Check locally.`, `launch: {driver: claude}`, `location: {type: directory, path: <P>}`, one key per line, with `<P>` written out.
- Make an agent folder with `A=$(mktemp -d /tmp/drive-attn-desktops-agent.XXXX) && echo "export A=$A" >> $DRIVE_ENV`, and save this fixture as `$A/.attn-mock-agent.json`, with `<A>` written out as the folder's path:

```json
{"version": 1, "turns": [
  {"includes": "move-shell", "actions": [
    {"type": "capture", "name": "shell", "pattern": "move-shell (\\S+)"},
    {"type": "capture", "name": "desk", "pattern": "move-shell \\S+ (\\S+)"},
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["terminal", "move", "{{shell}}", "{{desk}}"]}]},
  {"includes": "move-self", "actions": [
    {"type": "capture", "name": "desk", "pattern": "move-self (\\S+)"},
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["terminal", "move", "{{desk}}"]}]},
  {"includes": "delegate-one", "actions": [
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["delegate", "--agent", "codex", "--model", "mock-agent-1", "--brief", "Desktop drive delegate", "--cwd", "<A>", "--name", "desk-delegate"]}]}
]}
```

- Start a shell and an agent on the current desktop with `wsctl add-session --cwd $P --agent shell --label desk-shell --id desk-shell --place` and `wsctl add-session --cwd $A --agent claude --label desk-agent --id desk-agent --place`. In `wsctl list`, `<shell>` and `<agent>` are their `runtime_id` values. `<desktop>` is `current_desktop` in `bridge state '{"of":"window"}'`, and `<n>` is that desktop's `shortcut_slot` in `wsctl list`, 1 on a fresh instance. The steps below use slot 3 for another desktop.
- Session ids are spent once removed, so give each run's other shells new ids, such as `left-$(date +%s)`.

- **A desktop of its own.** Press the desktop 3 key: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"launcher.path.field"}}'` answers on the new empty desktop, and `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` lists a desktop with slot 3. `<d3>` is its id. Press the same key again: the window goes back to `<desktop>`, because the current desktop's digit returns to the previous desktop. Press it once more to come back.
- **Two shells side by side.** Run `wsctl add-session --cwd $P --agent shell --label left --id <left> --place`, then the same for `<right>`. Press `sidebar.session.<right>`. The panes in `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` give each shell's `pane_id`, `<L>` and `<R>`, and `bridge wait` on `tile.<L>` and `tile.<R>` answers with boxes side by side.
- **Spotlight and veil.** With `<R>` active, `bridge find` answers for `tile.<R>.spotlight.top`, the corner mark, and `tile.<L>.veil`, the veil over the left body. `bridge shot` shows the right header lighter with corners in the brand color, the left text dimmed, and no borders.
- **Focus style.** Run `bridge run '{"name":"ui.commandPalette"}'`, `bridge type '{"text":"Tile focus: rail"}'` and `bridge key '{"chord":"Enter"}'`: `bridge find` answers for `tile.<R>.rail`, the line down the active tile's left edge, and not for `tile.<R>.spotlight.top`. "Tile focus: dim" leaves neither mark, only the veil. The app keeps the style in its own settings: after `./attn instance stop-app` and a relaunch the style holds. Choose "Tile focus: spotlight" to restore the default.
- **Divider.** Run `bridge drag '{"target":{"key":"divider.row:<L>"},"by":[90,0]}'`: the receipt's `landed` names the divider, and `wsctl list` gives both leaves `wanted_cols`, the left one wider.
- **Zoom and focus.** Press zoom: `bridge wait` on `tile.<R>` answers wider than twice `tile.<L>`. Press it again and the widths return. Press focus: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"tile.<L>"},"gone":true}'` matches and the shot shows `<R>` alone with a border in the brand color. Press focus again and `tile.<L>` is back.
- **Tile header buttons.** Press `tile.<L>.focus` ("Focus agent left"): `<R>` leaves the screen and `tile.<L>.return` shows. Press it: both shells are back. An agent's header has no close button. A document's or browser tile's header has "Close tile", and a document's has "Focus document", which [markdown.md](markdown.md) drives.
- **Tile history.** Press `tile.<L>.header`, then history back: `active_pane_id` in `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` is `<R>`. History forward makes it `<L>` again, and each landing rings its tile once.
- **Row motion.** With four shells and two on screen, press the row key toward a tile off screen. Once `active_pane_id` in `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` names it, `bridge shot` shows the tile leaving the screen sliding out with the row at full opacity and the one joining sliding in from the other edge, rather than shrinking and growing in place. `bridge at_rest` answers once nothing moves or leaves, and the tree then holds only the screen's tiles. A shell that exits instead shrinks out in place.
- **The shelf.** With four shells and two on screen, `bridge find` answers for `shelf.chip.<leaf>` of each off-screen tile, and `bridge find` answers for `tile.<leaf>` only while that tile is on screen. Press a chip: `tile.<leaf>` shows beside the active tile. Press carry (`mod+alt+shift+right`): the active tile moves one place right and stays active. Press send to the shelf (`mod+alt+shift+up`): `tile.<leaf>` of the active tile goes and its chip shows. Press fewer tiles (`mod+alt+-`): `shelf.width.label` is named for one tile fewer, such as `1 tile`, and `./attn settings get narrowest_tile_cols` prints the new width. Press more tiles (`mod+alt+=`) to go back. Run `bridge hover '{"target":{"key":"shelf.chip.<leaf>"}}'`: `shelf.peek` shows the terminal's text and the tiles on screen stay. Run `bridge type '{"target":{"key":"shelf.peek.<leaf>.field"},"text":"echo peeked"}'` and `bridge key '{"chord":"Enter"}'`: `wsctl screen` for that terminal shows the line, and `shelf.peek` goes. The `native-shelf-*` scenarios drive the rest of the shelf: drags, caps, opening and closing, dragged widths and the peek's notices.
- **Pop out a terminal.** Hover `tile.<R>.header` and press `tile.<R>.popout` ("Pop out tile"): `tile.<R>` leaves the main window, `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` gives `<R>`'s leaf a `window`, and `bridge native_windows` lists `tile-window-1` beside `main`. `bridge` drives and reads the main window only, so work in the tile window with the platform's own input and capture: on Linux under `xvfb-run`, `xdotool windowfocus` the window id from `native_windows`, type with `xdotool type` and `xdotool key Return`, and capture it with `import -window <id>`. `wsctl screen --id <right>` shows the typed line and its output, and the capture shows the shell drawing in the window. The find key opens find there and its matches highlight in the window, not the main one. The palette's "Pop out tile" (`palette.row.command:tile-pop-out`) pops out the active tile the same way, and "Bring back tile" (`tile-bring-back`) brings a popped one back. The pop-out key pressed in the tile window brings `<R>` back: `bridge wait` on `tile.<R>` answers, `bridge state '{"of":"terminal:<right>"}'` holds what the window showed, and typing in the main window, after `bridge press` on `terminal.<right>`, reaches the shell again.
- **The overview.** Press the overview key: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"overview"}}'` answers, and the dialog is named "Desktop overview". Press `Right`: the dialog's active descendant moves to the next card, `overview.card.<id>`. Press `Enter`: `overview` goes and `current_desktop` in `bridge state '{"of":"window"}'` is that card's desktop. Open the overview again and press a slot's digit, such as `bridge key '{"chord":"3"}'`: the slot 3 desktop is current. Open it again and press `Escape`: `overview` goes. Open it again and press `overview.new`: a new desktop with the lowest free slot is current. Press the "Desktop overview" row, `sidebar.overview`, to open it from the sidebar.
- **Sidebar rename.** Run `bridge hover '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.desktop.<d3>"}}'` and `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.desktop.<d3>.rename"}}'`: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"desktop.rename.field"}}'` answers. Type `Drive` with `bridge type '{"target":{"key":"desktop.rename.field"},"text":"Drive"}'` and press `Enter`: `wsctl list` shows `<d3>` with `"name": "Drive"`.
- **Sort and Undo.** With named desktops in a hand order, run `bridge run '{"name":"ui.commandPalette"}'`, `bridge type '{"text":"Sort desktops"}'` and `bridge key '{"chord":"Enter"}'`: `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` lists numbered desktops by number, then named ones by name, and `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"notice.offer"}}'` answers "Desktops sorted Undo". Run `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"notice.offer"}}'`: the desktops return to the hand order and the notice reads "Desktops sorted ✓ Done". The notice stays while the pointer rests on it.
- **An agent moves a shell and itself.** Run `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "move-shell <shell> 3" --enter`. `./attn agent peek desk-agent` shows `<shell> moved to desktop <d3>`, and `wsctl list` holds that desktop, with `shortcut_slot` 3, whose `panes` include `<shell>`. Run `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "move-self 3" --enter`: peek shows `<agent> moved to desktop <d3>`, and `current_desktop` in `bridge state '{"of":"window"}'` stays `<desktop>`. Move by name with `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "move-self Drive" --enter`: peek shows `<agent> moved to desktop <d3>`.
- **An agent moves its delegate.** Move the agent back with `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "move-self <n>" --enter`. Run `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "delegate-one" --enter` and re-read `./attn agent peek desk-agent` until it shows the delegation's result with the delegate's `"session_id"`. `<delegate-terminal>` is the delegate's `runtime_id` in `wsctl list`. Run `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "move-shell <delegate-terminal> 5" --enter`, then the same with `<d3>`'s id from `wsctl list`: peek shows `<delegate-terminal> moved to desktop <id>` each time, and `wsctl list` shows the delegate's terminal on desktop 5, then on `<d3>`. Desktop 5 is left empty and unnamed, and the daemon removes it 30 seconds later.
- **Move with delegates.** In a folder whose `.attn-mock-agent.json` scripts a silent mock agent, start a `codex` dispatcher on a desktop of its own. Send the daemon's `delegate` command twice with it as `source_session_id` and `model` `mock-agent-1`, and once more from the first delegate, as `app/scripts/real-app-harness/scenario-native-move-with-delegates.mjs` does. Press the dispatcher's `tile.<pane>.header`, run `bridge run '{"name":"ui.commandPalette"}'` and `bridge type '{"text":"Move with delegates to <desktop>"}'`: `palette.row.command:move-with-delegates-<target>` is highlighted. The palette lists no such row while the agent on screen has no delegate on the current desktop. Press Enter: `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'` lists all four agents on `<target>`.
- **Close the desktop.** Run `bridge hover '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.desktop.<d3>"}}'` and `bridge press '{"target":{"key":"sidebar.desktop.<d3>.close"}}'`: `bridge wait '{"target":{"key":"close_desktop-dialog"}}'` answers named "Close Drive?", Cancel holds the keyboard, and nothing closes. The close key does nothing while the dialog shows, and Escape or Cancel leaves the desktop open. The palette's "Close desktop" asks the same for the current desktop. Press `close_desktop.confirm`: `<d3>` leaves `bridge state '{"of":"desktops"}'`, and `wsctl list` no longer lists its tiles. A desktop that holds a crew member's session does not ask. Its notice names the session.
- **The daemon removes empty desktops.** Press the key of a free slot, such as desktop 4, then go back to `<desktop>`. Re-read `wsctl list` until the new, unnamed desktop is gone, with a 45 second deadline that fails the run: the daemon removes it once it has been empty and not current for 30 seconds (`ATTN_EMPTY_DESKTOP_GRACE` shortens that, see Gotchas).
- **Launch desktop.** Run `./attn automation apply --file $P/check.yaml --launch-desktop own`. It prints the definition as JSON, its launch desktop labelled `Local check (no ⌘ number)`, and `wsctl list` holds a desktop named `Local check`. The desktop stays after 30 seconds while empty, because it is a launch desktop. `./attn automation set <id> --launch-desktop 5` prints the item with `desktop_5`, which `wsctl list` now names `Local check`. `--launch-desktop own` again makes another new desktop named `Local check`, and the earlier ones stay. `./attn automation run <id>` starts the run's session on the launch desktop. Set a crew member's launch desktop with `./attn crew set <member> --launch-desktop own` ([crew.md](crew.md)). A run that lands there is driven in [automations.md](automations.md).
- **What agents are told.** `./attn skill --reference terminal` covers moving terminals between desktops, with `attn terminal move <id> 8`.
- **The shell has no session.** Run `./attn terminal move <agent> 4` from the instance shell. It exits 1 with `terminal: caller rule: attn terminal requires a live calling agent; run it inside an attn agent session`.
- **The layout settings.** Run `./attn settings set narrowest_tile_cols wide`: it exits 1 with `settings set: daemon error: narrowest_tile_cols must be a positive whole number, got "wide"`. Press the settings key (`ui.openSettings`) and `settings.nav.general`: `settings.input.narrowest_tile_cols.field` shows 66. Press the field, press `mod+a`, type `100` and press `Enter`: `./attn settings get narrowest_tile_cols` prints `100` and fewer `tile.<leaf>` show. Set it back to 66.
- **A width with less room than it was saved at.** Send `wsctl request --json '{"cmd":"desktop_set_wanted_cols","request_id":"wide","desktop_id":"<desktop>","leaf_id":"<first leaf>","cols":120,"area_cols":400}' --event profile_action_result`: the neighbours stay on screen and `wantedCols` and `wantedAreaCols` stay in `tree_json`. Send it again with `"area_cols":0`, a width with no stored area: the neighbours leave when 120 cols leave them no room, and stay only when `cols` is wider than the area.
- **Pop out and bring back, over the protocol.** Send `wsctl request --json '{"cmd":"desktop_pop_out_leaf","request_id":"pop","desktop_id":"<desktop>","leaf_id":"<leaf>","frame":{"x":120,"y":80,"width":640,"height":480}}' --event profile_action_result`: `tree_json` in `wsctl list` gives `<leaf>` that `window` and keeps its place. Send it again with another frame to save a move. A `width` or `height` of 0 answers `invalid`, naming the frame. `desktop_show_leaf` and `desktop_show_session` of the popped leaf from another desktop make it active and leave `current_desktop_id` where it was, and closing a leaf in main never makes the popped leaf active. `desktop_bring_back_leaf` with the same ids clears `window`, and succeeds again on a leaf that is not popped.

## Contexts

- **Durability across restarts**: desktops, their names, slots, order, tiles and active tile are the daemon's records and come back after any restart, with every terminal in its tile. Tile history belongs to a window and starts empty in a new window.
- **Linux**: `attn terminal move` and launch desktops work the same where the daemon and CLI run on Linux. On Ubuntu 24.04, GNOME's defaults take Ctrl+Alt+Shift+arrows (`gsettings get org.gnome.desktop.wm.keybindings move-to-workspace-left`), so the carry and send keys reach attn only after a rebind on either side, and the palette's commands always work. Bindings there (the `LINUX` table in `native/crates/attn-app/src/keymap.rs`): Ctrl+Shift+1–9 switch, Ctrl+Alt+1–9 send and follow, Ctrl+Shift+Alt+1–9 send and stay, Ctrl+Shift+G overview, Ctrl+Shift+D and Ctrl+Alt+D split, Ctrl+Alt+Z zoom, Ctrl+Alt+Enter focus, Ctrl+Shift+W close, Ctrl+Shift+arrows move focus, Ctrl+Shift+[ and ] history, Ctrl+Shift+PageUp and PageDown step desktops, Ctrl+Alt+- and Ctrl+Alt+= step the shelf width, Ctrl+Alt+] and Ctrl+Alt+[ step the sidebar width, Ctrl+Alt+Shift+←→ carry the active column and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+↑ sends it to the shelf, and Ctrl+Shift+O pops out the active tile.
- **Outposts**: a terminal that runs on an outpost sits in a tile on a home desktop like any other, and moves the same way. While its host is offline the tile stays where it is and typing into it is not sent.
- **Profiles**: each profile has its own desktops and current desktop. Desktop refs in `attn terminal move`, `--launch-desktop` and callouts resolve only in the caller's profile, and terminals in another profile are not found.
- **Performance and idle cost**: once `bridge at_rest` answers, the app's tree holds only the tiles on screen. When a change touches tile layout or switching, measure it with `docs/internal/perf-testing.md`.
- **Keyboard and native input**: every desktop and tile action has a key, and the overview runs on arrows, ↵, ⇧↵, digits and Esc. File → "Close Pane" carries no key, so ⌘W is whatever `terminal.close` and `session.close` are bound to. After a switch, typing goes straight to the shown desktop's active tile.
- **Compatibility across versions**: a later version reads today's desktops through the store's migrations. An arrangement from before desktops becomes one desktop per old workspace at the first daemon start, before any shell. For the profile migration, see [profiles.md](profiles.md).
- **Flow**: in desktop flow the sidebar lists each desktop with its tiles, and ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ step through desktops. In queue flow the sidebar shows desktop chips and ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ walk the queue instead ([queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md)).
- **Accessibility**: the desktop overview keeps focus while the arrows move between cards, and names the focused card as its active descendant (`overview.card.<desktop>`), so VoiceOver reads each desktop as the arrows reach it.

## Gotchas

- Pressing the current desktop's digit goes back to the previous desktop, once the window has left one. A run that presses ⌘1 twice expecting to stay is wrong, not attn.
- The daemon removes a desktop with no name once it has been empty and not current for 30 seconds, numbered or not. Name it, put a tile on it, or make it a launch desktop before a step that comes back to it. The daemon reads the wait from `ATTN_EMPTY_DESKTOP_GRACE` (such as `2s`) when it starts. A short value shortens the sweep step.
- ⌘W on a terminal tile ends what runs in it: a split shell closes, and an agent's session closes too. Press it on a shell in a recipe, never on the agent you still need.
- `attn delegate` without a role needs `--model` and `--cwd`. The mock agent answers to `--model mock-agent-1`, and `--agent` defaults to the caller's harness. Without `--model` the delegation step stops at `--model is required`.
- `attn terminal` needs a session: from the instance shell it exits 1 with the caller rule. Drive moves through the mock agent.
- ⌘D and ⌘⇧D split only from a terminal tile. With a markdown, browser, seed or editor tile active they do nothing.
- `move-with-delegates-*` appears only while the agent on screen has a delegate on the current desktop. The baseline has no delegate until the delegation step runs.
- The overview's ⇧↵ and "Send focused pane here" do nothing for the current desktop's card.
- Working but wrong: ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ are labeled as desktop moves, with the queue meaning in parentheses, and in queue flow they walk the queue.
- The window opens at 1200 by 800, where one tile fits at 66 columns. Widen it with `bridge set_window_bounds` before a step that needs two tiles on screen.
- `bridge` reads and drives the main window only. A popped-out tile's window needs the platform's own input and capture.
