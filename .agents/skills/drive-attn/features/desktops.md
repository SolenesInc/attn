# Desktops and tiles

Desktops are arrangements of tiles, like macOS Spaces: switch, split, sort,
and move tiles between desktops. Each profile has its own desktops, and every
window shows the profile's current desktop. A tile is one rectangle on a
desktop: a terminal tile, or a browser, markdown, seed or editor tile. People
arrange desktops from the keyboard, the sidebar and the overview. Agents move
their own terminals and shells, and crew members and automations start their
sessions on a launch desktop.

## Sub-features

- `desktop-switch`: go to a desktop with ⌘1–⌘9, the sidebar, the overview or the palette. The current desktop's digit goes back to the previous desktop.
- `desktop-overview`: every desktop as a card, with keys to switch, send the active tile and create.
- `desktop-new`: create a desktop, which takes the lowest free number. ⌘<n> or a move to a free digit creates that numbered desktop.
- `desktop-rename`: name a desktop from the sidebar.
- `desktop-close`: close a desktop and everything on it, after a confirmation. ⌘W closes an empty desktop.
- `desktop-sort`: numbered desktops first, then names A–Z, with Undo. Drag a desktop in the sidebar to reorder it.
- `empty-desktop-sweep`: the daemon removes an unnamed desktop 30 seconds after the desktop is both empty and not current.
- `tile-split`: open a shell beside or below the active terminal tile.
- `tile-focus`: move the active tile with arrows. The focus style marks the active tile (dim, rail or spotlight).
- `tile-zoom`: zoom the active tile, or focus it alone on the desktop.
- `tile-move`: send the active tile to another desktop and follow or stay. Drag a tile. Agents move terminals. Move an agent with its delegates.
- `tile-history`: walk back and forward through the tiles this window showed.
- `tile-close`: close the active tile.
- `layout-settings`: the narrowest tile width in columns and the sidebar width, in Settings → Appearance ([sidebar.md](sidebar.md)).
- `shelf-row`: the screen shows a run of the row's columns and the shelf in the title bar lists the rest ([sidebar.md](sidebar.md)). ⌥⌘←→ slide along the row and leave for the next desktop past its end. ⌥⌘− and ⌥⌘= step the narrowest width until one fewer or one more tile fits, and ⌥⌘= clears the dragged widths on screen when they are what blocks another tile.
- `shelf-bring`: a chip, a cap's menu, ⌘J, the palette, a sidebar click and queue steps bring an off-screen tile on the current desktop beside the active tile. ⌘[ and ⌘] slide to a tile without reordering.
- `shelf-carry`: ⇧⌥⌘←→ carry the active column along the row.
- `shelf-send`: ⇧⌥⌘↑ sends the active tile to the shelf: a stacked tile to the row's end, a single tile past the screen's far side, or a held column when it would have fit.
- `shelf-open`: ⌘D, ⌘N and ⌘T open beside the active column. ⌘⇧D and ⌘⇧N open below it until the 320px height limit, then beside with a message.
- `shelf-close`: ⌘W, an agent's exit and `attn terminal close` go to the next tile in the stack, or else the previous one. For a single tile, they go to the next column, or else the previous one.
- `shelf-agent-terminals`: a terminal an agent starts goes to the row's end and takes no focus. `attn terminal open --down` stacks it below the agent.
- `shelf-documents`: a document an agent opens slides into view if the active tile stays on screen. Otherwise its chip counts as waiting and ⌘J takes it first. A document that arrives while its desktop is not mounted gets the same treatment when the user returns. So does a document, seed or browser tile already off screen that an agent opens again. Closing the document restores the screen.
- `shelf-drags`: a chip on a tile's center swaps the two columns. A chip on a tile's edge places or stacks its column, and between chips it reorders the row without taking focus. A tile dropped beside a chip moves to that side of the chip's column and comes on screen with it, active, clearing a dragged width of either that keeps them apart; when two narrowest tiles do not fit, it shows alone with a message; on a cap it goes beside the row's first or last column, and a stacked tile leaves its stack (the `shelf-drop-beside` scenario covers every drop place). A chip on a sidebar desktop moves its whole column there and follows it. Dragging a divider between columns sets wanted widths, past the narrowest width it moves the far column to the shelf, and dragging back restores it. Double-clicking a divider clears the widths beside it, and "Clear dragged widths" clears them across the desktop. Esc cancels any of these drags.
- `shelf-peek`: hovering a chip shows a panel of that terminal's latest text and a reply box, with no terminal mounted and no layout change. It opens only when the pointer moved onto the chip, never for one that layout brought under a still pointer. A stacked column's chip has one mark per tile, `[data-shelf-member="<leaf>"]`: moving onto a mark peeks that tile and underlines the mark, and the rest of the chip peeks the column's last-used tile without replacing a mark's peek. ↵ sends a typed reply and closes the panel, or opens the tile when the box is empty. When the agent has stopped or its outpost is not reachable, ↵ sends nothing: the box keeps the reply and a notice above it gives the reason. When the terminal does not confirm a sent reply, a toast says so and the reply is in the box the next time the panel opens. Open carries a reply that was never submitted to the tile's notice, which offers Copy and Discard. When Return failed, Open on a live terminal drops the copy, because the text is in the terminal's input; when the agent has stopped or its outpost is not reachable, the tile keeps it. An agent that stops makes a reply whose Return failed an unsent one: the tile shows it however the user reaches it, and after a resume ↵ in the panel sends the whole reply, never Return alone, and nothing goes out on its own. Esc closes the panel and discards a reply that was never submitted. Any on-screen browser tile hides while the panel shows. A document or browser tile's panel has only its title and kind.
- `shelf-motion`: a layout change moves tiles on one spring with one-shot Web Animations on `transform` and `opacity`. A slide moves every tile together, a new tile grows and fades in, and a closing tile shrinks and fades out, holding its terminal's last frame. Browser tiles snap, and a divider drag, focus mode and `prefers-reduced-motion` never animate.
- `launch-desktop`: the desktop where a crew member's or an automation's sessions start.

## How to get to it

### People

- Keys (`app/src/shortcuts/registry.ts`): ⌘1–⌘9 (`desktop.select1`–`desktop.select9`), ⌥⌘1–⌥⌘9 move the active tile and follow (`desktop.send1`–`desktop.send9`), ⇧⌥⌘1–⇧⌥⌘9 move it and stay (`desktop.sendStay1`–`desktop.sendStay9`), ⌘G (`desktop.overview`), ⌘D and ⌘⇧D split (`terminal.splitVertical`, `terminal.splitHorizontal`), ⌘⇧Z zoom (`terminal.toggleZoom`), ⌘⇧↩ focus (`terminal.toggleMaximize`), ⌥⌘←→↑↓ (`terminal.focusLeft|Right|Up|Down`), ⌥⌘− and ⌥⌘= (`shelf.fewerTiles`, `shelf.moreTiles`), ⇧⌥⌘←→ (`shelf.carryLeft`, `shelf.carryRight`), ⇧⌥⌘↑ (`shelf.sendToShelf`) and the sidebar width's ⌥⌘] and ⌥⌘[ ([sidebar.md](sidebar.md)), ⌘W (`terminal.close`, `session.close`), ⌘[ and ⌘] (`session.historyBack`, `session.historyForward`), and in desktop flow ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ (`session.prev`, `session.next`).
- Native menu (`app/src-tauri/core/src/lib.rs`): File → "Close Pane" carries ⌘W. Close Pane closes the focused markdown, seed, editor or browser tile. Otherwise Close Pane closes the terminal on screen and ends the terminal's shell or agent. On an empty desktop, Close Pane closes the desktop.
- Palette (`app/src/application/useAppCommands.tsx`): `desktop-overview`, `new-desktop`, `close-desktop`, `sort-desktops`, `desktop-<id>` ("Go to <desktop>"), `move-with-delegates-<id>` ("Move with delegates to <desktop>", offered while the agent on screen has a delegate on this desktop), and `tile-focus-dim`, `tile-focus-rail`, `tile-focus-spotlight`, and `shelf-fewer-tiles`, `shelf-more-tiles`, `shelf-carry-left`, `shelf-carry-right`, `shelf-send-to-shelf`, `shelf-clear-dragged-widths`.
- Sidebar (`app/src/components/SidebarDesktops.tsx`): the "Desktop overview" row, each desktop's row ("Open <desktop>"), its "Rename <desktop>" and "Close <desktop>" buttons, dragging a desktop's row to reorder it, and dragging an agent's row onto another desktop. Dropping the row with ⌥ held brings the agent's delegates along. Each tile's row under its desktop has a "Close <title>" button, and a browser tile's row has a "Reload <title>" button (`app/src/components/SidebarRows.tsx`). The row buttons take clicks only while the pointer is over the row. Closing a desktop shows a confirmation first (`app/src/components/DesktopClosePrompt.tsx`).
- Overview (`app/src/components/DesktopOverview.tsx`): arrows move, ↵ switches, ⇧↵ sends the active tile, digits switch, Esc closes, "+ New desktop" creates.
- Tile headers (`app/src/components/SessionTerminalDesktop/DesktopDockTile.tsx`, `DesktopAgentHeader.tsx`): "Close tile", "Focus agent <name>", and dragging a header to another place or desktop (`app/src/application/useLeafDrag.ts`). An agent tile's header has two rows of one fixed height: the state dot, the title and the settling and ask signals, then the seed, role, outpost, usage and pull request or automation. As the tile narrows, each row shortens its pieces in a fixed order (`headerFit.ts`); the dot, the title and the signals stay. "Rename session" and "Focus agent" appear over the end of the title on hover. A focused tile shows no bar above it; its header shows "Return to row" instead, at the end of an agent's first row and among a tile's actions. With two or more review documents focused, a row of their tabs sits above the tile.
- Shelf (`app/src/components/SessionTerminalDesktop/DesktopShelf.tsx`, in `TitleBar`, inside the queue bar when the queue sidebar is collapsed): each chip, the `‹ n` and `n ›` caps and their menus, the live dots, hovering the dots for the width control, hovering a chip for the peek panel (`ShelfPeekPanel.tsx`), dragging a chip, and dropping a tile on the shelf (`shelfDropTarget.ts`, `useDesktopLeafDrag.ts`). Column dividers are on the tile area (`DesktopLayoutRenderer.tsx`).
- Launch desktop field in the crew launch tab and the automation form (`app/src/components/LaunchDesktopSelect.tsx`).
- Where people see what agents do: the sidebar lists a terminal that an agent opens or moves under the desktop the terminal sits on. An empty desktop shows the launcher (`app/src/application/EmptyDesktopLauncher.tsx`).
- Their own shell: the `--launch-desktop` verbs below. `attn terminal` refuses without a session.

### Agents

- `attn terminal move [<terminal>] <desktop>` (`cmd/attn/terminal.go`): moves the caller's terminal, or a shell, or an agent below the caller in the caller's delegation chain. `<desktop>` is a digit, a name or an id. The command creates a missing numbered desktop. `attn terminal open --desktop <ref>` is in [terminals.md](terminals.md).
- `attn crew set <member> --launch-desktop` (`cmd/attn/crew.go`) and `attn automation set <id> --launch-desktop`, `attn automation apply --file <yaml> --launch-desktop` (`cmd/attn/automation.go`).
- `attn delegate --desktop` places a delegate ([delegation.md](delegation.md)), and `attn callout show desktop:<which>` points at a desktop ([callouts.md](callouts.md)).
- Prompts: placement, reach and moves in `attn skill --reference terminal` (`internal/prompts/content/skills/attn/references/terminal.md`).

### Triggers

- The daemon removes a desktop once it has been empty and not the current desktop for 30 seconds, if the desktop is also unnamed and not any item's launch desktop (`internal/daemon/empty_desktops.go`).
- A crew wake or an automation run starts the session on the member's or the automation's launch desktop (`internal/daemon/launch_desktops.go`). Crew and automations own the wake and the run.

## Driving it

Preconditions:

- Use the baseline instance with its app. Check that `bridge --launch --wait-ready ping` answers, then run `bridge dismiss_whats_new` to dismiss What's new.
- Make a proof folder with `P=$(mktemp -d /tmp/drive-attn-desktops.XXXX) && echo "export P=$P" >> $DRIVE_ENV`, and save `check.yaml` in it: `api_version: attn.dev/automations/v1alpha1`, `name: Local check`, `trigger: {type: manual}`, `prompt: Check locally.`, `launch: {driver: claude}`, `location: {type: directory, path: <P>}`, one key per line, with `<P>` written out.
- Make an agent folder with `A=$(mktemp -d /tmp/drive-attn-desktops-agent.XXXX) && echo "export A=$A" >> $DRIVE_ENV`, and save this fixture as `$A/.attn-mock-agent.json`, with `<A>` written out as the folder's path:

```json
{"version": 1, "turns": [
  {"includes": "move-shell", "actions": [
    {"type": "capture", "name": "shell", "pattern": "move-shell (\\S+)"},
    {"type": "capture", "name": "desk", "pattern": "move-shell \\S+ (\\S+)"},
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["terminal", "move", "{{shell}}", "{{desk}}"]}]},
  {"includes": "move-self", "actions": [
    {"type": "capture", "name": "desk", "pattern": "move-self (\\S+)"},
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["terminal", "move", "{{desk}}"]}]},
  {"includes": "delegate-one", "actions": [
    {"type": "attn", "args": ["delegate", "--agent", "codex", "--model", "mock-agent-1", "--brief", "Desktop drive delegate", "--cwd", "<A>", "--name", "desk-delegate"]}]}
]}
```

- Start a shell and an agent on the current desktop with `wsctl add-session --cwd $P --agent shell --label desk-shell --id desk-shell --place` and `wsctl add-session --cwd $A --agent claude --label desk-agent --id desk-agent --place`. In `wsctl list`, `<shell>` and `<agent>` are their `runtime_id` values. `<desktop>` is `arrangement.currentDesktopId` in `bridge get_state`, and `<n>` is that desktop's `slot` there (its `shortcut_slot` in `wsctl list`), 1 on a fresh instance. The steps below use slots 2 and 3 for the other desktops.

- **Split with the keyboard.** Select the shell for setup with `bridge select_session '{"sessionId":"desk-shell"}'`. Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"d","modifiers":["command"]}'`. `bridge get_desktop '{"sessionId":"desk-shell"}'` lists one more entry in `panes`, and `wsctl list` shows a new `"agent": "shell"` entry on `<desktop>`. Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"d","modifiers":["command","shift"]}'` with the new shell active: a third shell opens, and `bridge get_session_ui_state '{"sessionId":"desk-shell"}'` lists splits of both kinds in `desktop.splits`, one with `direction: "vertical"` and one with `direction: "horizontal"`. The ids of the two new shells are `sessions[].id` in `wsctl list`, selecting entries whose `agent` is `shell` other than `desk-shell`: call them `<split-1>` and `<split-2>`.
- **Move focus, zoom and focus.** Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"ArrowLeft","modifiers":["command","option"]}'`: `desktop.view.activeLeafId` in `get_session_ui_state` names another tile. Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"z","modifiers":["command","shift"]}'`: `desktop.view.zoomedPaneId` names the active tile. Press the key again, and `zoomedPaneId` is null. Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"Enter","modifiers":["command","shift"]}'`: `desktop.view.maximizedPaneId` names the active tile. Press the key again, and `maximizedPaneId` is null.
- **Close a tile.** Select a split shell, never `desk-shell`, with `bridge select_session '{"sessionId":"<split-2>"}'`, and run `bridge native_key '{"key":"w","modifiers":["command"]}'`. The File menu takes ⌘W (`handledBy: ["menu"]`) and closes that shell with its tile. `bridge get_desktop '{"sessionId":"desk-shell"}'` lists one entry fewer in `panes`, `wsctl list` no longer holds `<split-2>`, and `desk-shell` keeps its text (`wsctl screen --id <shell>`).
- **Walk off an empty desktop.** On an empty desktop, with the launcher's path input focused, run `bridge native_key '{"key":"ArrowRight","modifiers":["command","option"]}'`: `arrangement.currentDesktopId` in `bridge get_state` names the next desktop. ArrowLeft goes to the previous one. In desktop flow ArrowUp and ArrowDown do the same; in queue flow they step through the queue.
- **Close other tiles with ⌘W.** Open one tile of each kind: a markdown tile from a mock agent whose fixture runs `attn open notes.md`, a browser tile from one that runs `attn browser open <url>`, the palette's "Open Editor tile" (`notebook` in `tree_json`), and a seed tile from "Open as tile" on a seed's page in the garden. For each tile, click the tile natively so it holds keyboard focus, then press ⌘W. Read the desktop's `tree_json` in `wsctl list` until the tile is gone. The agent that opened the tile stays. Then press ⌘<n> for an empty numbered desktop and press ⌘W there: the desktop closes at once.
- **New desktop from the palette.** Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"k","modifiers":["command","shift"]}'`, `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"[role=\"combobox\"][aria-label=\"Commands\"]","focused":true,"timeoutMs":5000}'`, `bridge native_text '{"text":"New desktop"}'` and `bridge native_key '{"key":"Enter"}'`. `bridge get_state` shows one more entry in `arrangement.desktops` with the lowest free `slot`, `arrangement.currentDesktopId` is the new one, and `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"[data-testid=\"empty-desktop-launcher\"]","timeoutMs":5000}'` matches.
- **Switch with digits and go back.** Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"<n>","modifiers":["command"]}'`: `arrangement.currentDesktopId` is `<desktop>`. Press the key again: the new desktop is current, and `previousDesktopId` is `<desktop>`. Press the key once more to return.
- **The daemon removes empty desktops.** Re-read `bridge get_state` until the new, unnamed desktop is gone from `arrangement.desktops`, with a 45 second deadline that fails the run: the daemon removes it once it has been empty and not current for 30 seconds (`ATTN_EMPTY_DESKTOP_GRACE` shortens that, see Gotchas). `wsctl list` no longer holds it either.
- **Send and stay.** Select the shell for setup. Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"2","modifiers":["command","option","shift"]}'`. `wsctl list` shows `desk-shell` under the desktop whose `shortcut_slot` is 2. The move creates that desktop if it was missing. `arrangement.currentDesktopId` is still `<desktop>`.
- **Step through desktops.** Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"ArrowDown","modifiers":["command"]}'`: `arrangement.currentDesktopId` is the entry after `<desktop>` in `arrangement.desktops`. After the last entry, it is the first entry. Run the same command with `ArrowUp`: `arrangement.currentDesktopId` is `<desktop>` again. Open the palette, type `Go to Desktop 2`, press Enter: the slot 2 desktop is current. Press ⌘<n> to return to `<desktop>`.
- **The overview.** Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"g","modifiers":["command"]}'` and `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"dialog.desktop-overview","timeoutMs":5000}'`. `bridge dom_text '{"selector":"dialog.desktop-overview h2"}'` names the profile and counts its desktops. Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"2"}'`: the overview closes and `currentDesktopId` is the slot 2 desktop. Open the overview again and press Escape: `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"dialog.desktop-overview","absent":true,"timeoutMs":5000}'` matches. Open the overview again and press ← and ↵: the overview closes and the desktop on the card to the left is current. Open the overview from the palette with the command `Desktop overview`, then click `dialog.desktop-overview .desktop-overview-card.new` ("+ New desktop") natively: a new desktop with the lowest free slot is current. Switch back to `<desktop>`, select a split shell, and open the overview. Move to the new desktop's card with → and press ⇧↵: `wsctl list` shows the shell on the new desktop and `currentDesktopId` stays on `<desktop>`. Open the overview from the sidebar by clicking the "Desktop overview" row natively, then close the overview with Escape.
- **Send and follow.** On the slot 2 desktop, select the shell and run `bridge native_key '{"key":"<n>","modifiers":["command","option"]}'`. The shell is back on `<desktop>` and `currentDesktopId` follows it. The slot 2 desktop is now empty and unnamed, and the daemon removes it 30 seconds later.
- **Tile history.** Select the agent, then the shell, for setup. Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"[","modifiers":["command"]}'`: `bridge get_state` shows `activeLeaf.sessionId` back on `desk-agent`. Run `bridge native_key '{"key":"]","modifiers":["command"]}'`: it is `desk-shell` again.
- **Focus style.** Open the palette, type `Tile focus`, and note which command is described as "Current tile focus style". Press Escape. Open the palette, type `Tile focus: dim`, and press Enter. Open it again and type the full `Tile focus: dim` title: `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":".unified-palette-option","textIncludes":"Current tile focus style","timeoutMs":5000}'` matches that row. Press Escape, repeat with `Tile focus: spotlight`, then restore the style you noted. A full title narrows the check to one row.
- **An agent moves a shell and itself.** Run `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "move-shell <shell> 3" --enter`. `./attn agent peek desk-agent` shows `<shell> moved to desktop <d3>`, and `wsctl list` holds that desktop, with `shortcut_slot` 3, whose `panes` include `<shell>`. Run `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "move-self 3" --enter`: peek shows `<agent> moved to desktop <d3>`, and `bridge get_state` keeps `arrangement.currentDesktopId` on `<desktop>`.
- **Move with delegates.** Move the agent back with `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "move-self <n>" --enter`. Run `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "delegate-one" --enter` and re-read `./attn agent peek desk-agent` until it shows the delegation's result with the delegate's `"session_id"`. `wsctl list` then shows that delegate's tile on `<desktop>`. Select the agent for setup, open the palette and type `Move with delegates to Desktop 3`. `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":".unified-palette-option[aria-selected=\"true\"]","textIncludes":"Move with delegates to Desktop 3","timeoutMs":5000}'` matches. Press Enter: `wsctl list` shows the agent and its delegate both on `<d3>`.
- **An agent moves its delegate.** `<delegate-terminal>` is the delegate's `runtime_id` in `wsctl list`. Run `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "move-shell <delegate-terminal> 5" --enter`, then the same with `<d3>`'s id from `wsctl list`: peek shows `<delegate-terminal> moved to desktop <id>` each time, and `wsctl list` shows the delegate's terminal on desktop 5, then on `<d3>`. Desktop 5 is left empty and unnamed, and the daemon removes it 30 seconds later.
- **Drag.** Points come from `bridge dom_native_point`. Drag `[data-testid="sidebar-session-desk-agent"]` onto the `<desktop>` row, `[data-desktop-id="<desktop>"] .sidebar-row-select`, with `bridge native_mouse '{"action":"drag","x":<x1>,"y":<y1>,"toX":<x2>,"toY":<y2>,"modifiers":["option"]}'`: `wsctl list` shows the agent and its delegate back on `<desktop>`. Drag the agent's row onto the `<d3>` row without a modifier: only the agent moves. Drag the agent's row back onto the `<desktop>` row the same way. Next, drag the `<desktop>` row without a modifier onto the lower edge of the whole `<d3>` group, which includes its tile rows. Target `[data-testid="sidebar-desktop-<d3>"]` with `"at":{"x":0.5,"y":0.9}`. The order of `desktops` in `wsctl list` changes. Drag the agent's header, `[data-pane-session-id="desk-agent"] .desktop-pane-header`, from an empty spot on the header onto the delegate tile's right edge: `tree_json` of `<desktop>` in `wsctl list` differs from before. Drag the delegate's header onto the `<d3>` sidebar row: `wsctl list` shows the delegate on `<d3>`.
- **Sort from the palette.** With the order the drag left, open the palette, type `Sort desktops`, press Enter. `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":".toast","textIncludes":"Desktops sorted","timeoutMs":5000}'` matches, and `wsctl list` orders numbered desktops first. Click the toast's Undo natively (point of `.toast-row--action button` from `bridge dom_native_point`): `wsctl list` returns to the earlier order.
- **Sidebar rows.** Click a desktop's row, `[data-desktop-id="<id>"] .sidebar-row-select` ("Open <desktop>"), natively: `currentDesktopId` is that desktop. With a browser tile open on `<desktop>` from a mock agent, move the pointer over its row, `.desktop-tile-item[data-tile-kind="browser"]`, and click its `.reload-session-btn` natively: the page server logs a second request. Move over the row again and click `.close-session-btn`: the tile leaves `tree_json`.
- **Tile header buttons.** Click `[data-pane-session-id="desk-agent"] [aria-label="Focus agent desk-agent"]` natively: `desktop.view.maximizedPaneId` in `get_session_ui_state` names its tile. Click its `[aria-label="Return to row"]` natively: `maximizedPaneId` is null. Click a browser tile's `[aria-label="Close tile"]` natively: the tile leaves `tree_json`.
- **Sidebar rename and close.** The sidebar's rename and close buttons take clicks only while the pointer is over the desktop's row, so each click starts on the row, as the README says. To click one, resolve the point of the row, `[data-desktop-id="<d3>"] .sidebar-row-select`, with `bridge dom_native_point` and move there with `bridge native_mouse '{"action":"move","x":<x>,"y":<y>}'`. Then resolve the button's point and click it with `bridge native_mouse '{"action":"click","x":<x>,"y":<y>}'`. Click `[data-testid="rename-desktop-<d3>"]` this way. Wait for the selected name field with `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"input[aria-label=\"Rename desktop\"]","focused":true,"timeoutMs":5000}'`, then run `bridge native_text '{"text":"Drive"}'` and `bridge native_key '{"key":"Enter"}'`. `wsctl list` shows `<d3>` with `"name": "Drive"`. Move the agent there by that name with `wsctl input --id <agent> --text "move-self Drive" --enter`: peek shows `<agent> moved to desktop <d3>`. Click `[data-testid="close-desktop-<d3>"]` the same way, moving over the row first: `bridge dom_wait '{"selector":"#desktop-close-title","textIncludes":"Close Drive?","timeoutMs":5000}'` matches. Click `.desktop-close-prompt .mp-button.primary` natively: `<d3>` and its tiles are gone from `wsctl list`, the shell with them.
- **Close from the palette.** Press ⌘3: a new empty desktop opens. Open the palette, type `Close desktop`, press Enter: the empty desktop closes at once, with no prompt, and leaves `arrangement.desktops`.
- **Launch desktop.** Run `./attn automation apply --file $P/check.yaml --launch-desktop own`. It prints the definition as JSON, its launch desktop labelled `Local check (no ⌘ number)`, and `wsctl list` holds a desktop named `Local check`. The desktop stays after 30 seconds while empty, because it is a launch desktop. `./attn automation set <id> --launch-desktop 5` prints the item with `desktop_5`, which `wsctl list` now names `Local check`. `--launch-desktop own` again makes another new desktop named `Local check`, and the earlier ones stay. `./attn automation run <id>` starts the run's session on the launch desktop.

## Contexts

- **Durability across restarts**: desktops, their names, slots, order, tiles and active tile are the daemon's records and come back after any restart, with every terminal in its tile. Tile history belongs to a window and starts empty in a new window.
- **Linux**: `attn terminal move` and launch desktops work the same where the daemon and CLI run on Linux. On Ubuntu 24.04, GNOME's defaults take Ctrl+Alt+Shift+arrows (`gsettings get org.gnome.desktop.wm.keybindings move-to-workspace-left`), so the carry and send keys reach attn only after a rebind on either side, and the palette's commands always work. Bindings there (`LINUX_SHORTCUTS`): Ctrl+Shift+1–9 switch, Ctrl+Alt+1–9 send and follow, Ctrl+Shift+Alt+1–9 send and stay, Ctrl+Shift+G overview, Ctrl+Shift+D and Ctrl+Alt+D split, Ctrl+Alt+Z zoom, Ctrl+Alt+Enter focus, Ctrl+Shift+W close, Ctrl+Shift+arrows move focus, Ctrl+Shift+[ and ] history, Ctrl+Shift+PageUp and PageDown step desktops, Ctrl+Alt+- and Ctrl+Alt+= step the shelf width, Ctrl+Alt+] and Ctrl+Alt+[ step the sidebar width, Ctrl+Alt+Shift+←→ carry the active column and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+↑ sends it to the shelf.
- **Outposts**: a terminal that runs on an outpost sits in a tile on a home desktop like any other, and moves the same way. While its host is offline the tile stays where it is and typing into it is not sent.
- **Profiles**: each profile has its own desktops and current desktop. Desktop refs in `attn terminal move`, `--launch-desktop` and callouts resolve only in the caller's profile, and terminals in another profile are not found.
- **Performance and idle cost**: the app keeps only the current desktop, the previous desktop and a drag's source desktop mounted, and a hidden terminal releases its drawing surface. `app/e2e/split-paint.spec.ts` checks repainting while splitting. When a change touches tile layout or switching, measure it with `docs/internal/perf-testing.md`.
- **Keyboard and native input**: every desktop and tile action has a key, and the overview runs on arrows, ↵, ⇧↵, digits and Esc. ⌘W reaches attn through File → "Close Pane", so rebinding `terminal.close` or `session.close` does not change the menu's ⌘W. After a switch, typing goes straight to the shown desktop's active tile.
- **Compatibility across versions**: a later version reads today's desktops through the store's migrations. An arrangement from before desktops opens the migration picker before any shell (scenario `desktop-migration`). For the profile migration, see [profiles.md](profiles.md).
- **Flow**: in desktop flow the sidebar lists each desktop with its tiles, and ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ step through desktops. In queue flow the sidebar shows desktop chips and ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ walk the queue instead ([queue-flow.md](queue-flow.md)).

## Gotchas

- Pressing the current desktop's digit goes back to the previous desktop, once the window has left one. A run that presses ⌘1 twice expecting to stay is wrong, not attn.
- The daemon removes a desktop with no name once it has been empty and not current for 30 seconds, numbered or not. Name it, put a tile on it, or make it a launch desktop before a step that comes back to it. The daemon reads the wait from `ATTN_EMPTY_DESKTOP_GRACE` (such as `2s`) when it starts. A short value shortens the sweep step.
- ⌘W on a terminal tile ends what runs in it: a split shell closes, and an agent's session closes too. Press it on a shell in a recipe, never on the agent you still need.
- `attn delegate` without a role needs `--model` and `--cwd`. The mock agent answers to `--model mock-agent-1`, and `--agent` defaults to the caller's harness. Without `--model` the delegation step stops at `--model is required`.
- `attn terminal` needs a session: from the instance shell it exits 1 with the caller rule. Drive moves through the mock agent.
- On a fresh app, What's new captures key presses. Dismiss it first.
- ⌘D and ⌘⇧D split only from a terminal tile. With a markdown, browser, seed or editor tile active they do nothing.
- `move-with-delegates-*` appears only while the agent on screen has a delegate on the current desktop. The baseline has no delegate until the delegation step runs.
- The overview's ⇧↵ and "Send focused pane here" do nothing for the current desktop's card.
- `bridge capture_screenshot` leaves out terminal content. Prove what a moved terminal still holds with `bridge read_pane_text` or `wsctl screen --id <terminal>`.
- Working but wrong: ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ are labeled as desktop moves, with the queue meaning in parentheses, and in queue flow they walk the queue.
- The parked window starts at 1200px, where one tile fits at 66 columns. Set the width to 1900px before a step that needs two tiles on screen.
- A chip's peek panel can cover what you click next. Move over a tile header and wait for `[data-testid="shelf-peek"]` to leave, or press Escape, before the next drag.
- A dragged desktop drops between whole desktop groups. The lower edge of a populated desktop's header is above its tiles, so dropping there can leave the order unchanged. Use the group's lower edge.
- The second row of an agent's tile header holds its "Delegation chain" button once it has delegates. A drag that starts there does nothing. Start header drags from an empty spot.
- A selector such as `[data-tile-kind="markdown"]` matches the tile's sidebar row before the tile. Clicking that row selects the tile. That is enough for ⌘W.
- A seed tile opened with "Open as tile" shows as the active tile while keyboard focus stays on the app, so ⌘W does nothing until the tile is clicked (seed `focus-seed-tile-opened-garden-so`, s-r374ak).
- `dom_wait` checks the first matching element. Search the full focus-style title before checking its description.
