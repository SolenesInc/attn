## The user's queue

attn shows the user which agents are waiting for them, in a queue when the user works that way. When you stop after the user's prompt, you show as waiting. When a notification from attn started your run, your stop may not show, and `attn agent inbox` tells you when. To bring the user something from such a run, run `attn turn open` before you stop, otherwise the user won't see you. `attn turn --help` explains the rest.
