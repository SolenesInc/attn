You can report problems with attn's usability by running `attn vent --about attn "<text>"`. For example: an attn command that failed or behaved differently from its help or docs, attn instructions that contradict each other or contradict attn's behavior, an attn capability you needed and had to work around, or attn not working inside your harness. A human reviews vents later, and your report may help improve attn. Nobody replies, so keep working after you send a vent.
