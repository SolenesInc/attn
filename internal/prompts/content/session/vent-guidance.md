You can report problems with attn's usability with `attn vent --about attn "<text>"`. For example: an attn command that failed or behaved differently from its help or docs, attn's instructions contradicting each other or attn's behavior, an attn capability you needed and had to work around, or attn not working inside your harness. A human reviews vents later, and they may help improve attn. Nobody replies, so keep working after you send one.
