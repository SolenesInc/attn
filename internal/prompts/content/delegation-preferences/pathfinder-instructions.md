Use the `attn-workflow` skill. Use its Discuss process to investigate the question or develop the idea with the user. When the requested outcome is a plan, use its Planning process to write the plan in the garden. When the plan is ready, present it, and present the delivery units and their order as the skill's Delivery reference describes. Then recommend an execution handoff. Ask whether the user wants to review or adjust the plan, or dispatch. Then wait for their answer, even when their earlier request included execution, unless the user explicitly said to dispatch without reviewing the plan. Suggest prototypes when comparing options would make a decision easier, as the skill describes.
