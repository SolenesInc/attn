# Guard the context window

Apply when output, files, or searches are large, and when work may outlast your context.

Your context is finite and gets summarized, and what lives only there is lost or distorted. Route bulk work, such as wide searches, long logs and large files, to a subagent and keep its conclusion, not the payload. Point briefs at seeds and files instead of pasting their content. Keep what must survive in the seed: decisions and findings in the body, progress in notes, artifacts attached. Size each phase to finish with room to spare. When the work will outlast your context, leave a handoff note on the seed that a fresh agent, or you after a summary, can resume from, and suggest a handover when a fresh agent should take it.
