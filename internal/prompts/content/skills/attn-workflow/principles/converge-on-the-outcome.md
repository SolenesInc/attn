# Converge on the outcome

Apply to a planned rewrite or migration with explicit phases.

In a planned rewrite or migration, aim at the verified end state, not at keeping every intermediate step smooth; compatibility code written only to keep a midpoint working tends to outlive the migration. Declare in the plan where intermediate breakage is allowed under [Delivery](../references/delivery.md), keep the checks for the areas being changed running throughout, and verify everything at the end. Each delivered part leaves the product working.
