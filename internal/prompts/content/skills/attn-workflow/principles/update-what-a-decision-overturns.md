# Update what a decision overturns

Apply when the user's decision contradicts the repository's docs, code, or agent guidance.

When the user's decision contradicts the repository's docs, code, or agent guidance, change them as part of the task. When the change falls outside the task, plant a seed for it that names the decision. Until those statements change, they teach what the decision overturned to every agent that reads them, including you once your context is summarized.
