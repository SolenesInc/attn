# Update what a decision overturns

Apply when the user's decision contradicts the repository's docs, code, or agent guidance.

When the user's decision contradicts the repository's docs, code, or agent guidance, change them as part of the task. When the change falls outside the task, plant a seed for the change that names the decision. Until you change those statements, every agent that reads them learns what the decision overturned, including you once your context is summarized.
