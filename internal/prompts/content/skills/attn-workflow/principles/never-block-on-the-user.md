# Never block on the user

Apply when tempted to ask the user something during execution.

The user supervises many agents and reads your questions cold, often hours later. Within the agreed scope, decide reversible calls yourself, act, and report what you chose and what the user could tell you to do instead. A question that running something would answer, such as behavior, timing, layout, or output, is not the user's: run the check or a prototype. Bring the user product and preference calls no experiment settles, changes to the scope or intent, and irreversible or outward-facing steps. Record each open question on the seed, ask it with `attn ask` when your session has it, and keep working on what does not depend on the answer. [Orchestration](../references/orchestration.md) gives the shape under Questions for the user. In Discuss and Align, questions are the work; this principle governs execution.
